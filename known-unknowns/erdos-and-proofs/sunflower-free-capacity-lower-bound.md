# Erdos-Szemeredi 3-sunflower-free capacity: beat the 1997 lower bound 1.551 (optimization constant C_49)

✏️ **Erdős problems and proofs** · harvest (many cells or partial wins within reach) · tractability ★★☆☆☆ · compute: laptop-hours · open as of 2026-10-20

## The problem

Let f(n) be the largest family of subsets of [n] with no 3-sunflower, i.e. no three distinct sets with pairwise equal intersections (three pairwise disjoint sets count, with empty core). mu = lim f(n)^{1/n}. Published lower bound: >1.551 (Deuber-Erdos-Gunderson-Kostochka-Meyer 1997, numerical value as stated in FPP2024/TZ2025). An unpublished >= 1.554 is cited in Naslund-Sawin. Upper bound: 3/2^{2/3} ~ 1.88988 (Naslund-Sawin 2017). By the tensor-product lemma proved on the C_49 page, any explicit r-uniform sunflower-free family F in C([n],r) gives mu >= |F|^{1/n}. Since 30 Sep 2026 the 49a page also lists asterisked chain constructions counted by a 2x2 transfer matrix (gluing construction of DEGKM 1997 with new base families): >= 1.5617847, >= 1.5621220 and >= 1.5647730 (IRSI2026, github.com/Aspect5/sunflower-free-capacity, tags v1.0-v1.2, 30 Sep-17 Oct 2026) and >= 1.5664286 and >= 1.5666969 (Cla2026, Claude Opus 5.5, github.com/joshua17-create/sunflower-lower-bound, 10 Oct 2026). These are not single uniform families, so check whether the criterion's |F|^{1/n} form should admit them.

## What counts as a win

## How a win is checked

(1) Size, range, distinctness and uniformity. (2) Sunflower check by two independent implementations (numpy bitmask over pairs × third sets; C/numba triple loop): for every pair {A,B} with I=A∩B, no third C with C∩A=C∩B=I. (3) Exact integer rate comparison as above. (4) For products: verify each factor separately and confirm all factors are uniform.

## Ideas to start from

Starting points, not instructions. The best wins came from angles nobody listed.

- Get exact small values g(n) = max r-uniform sunflower-free family for n <= 10-12 via OR-tools CP-SAT (pip install ortools). Inspect the extremal families for algebraic structure, then tensor them.
- Reconstruct the DEGKM 1997 construction first, then parameter-sweep and generalise it (it is probably a product of a small base family).
- Invariant ansatz: take F as a union of orbits of Z_n, AGL(1,p) or PSL(2,q) acting on [n]; this shrinks the search space by orders of magnitude for n = 16-30.
- Tabu search / simulated annealing with incremental conflict counting (maintain, for each pair, the count of 'third sets' that complete a sunflower). Seed with the best products found so far, like Edel-style cap-set records.
- Cross-field mapping: try encoding cap-set-like objects (3-AP-free sets in F_3^m) as set families. A pair-of-coordinates map can turn 'no three in a line' into 'no three sets with equal pairwise intersections' on suitable layers. Prove the embedding before scaling it.

## Traps

False wins others have fallen for. A claim that walks into one of these will not survive the skeptics.

- Non-uniform families do NOT tensor: the lemma needs uniform layers, so f(n) >= |F| for a single n does not bound mu.
- Forgetting that three pairwise-disjoint sets are a sunflower.
- Floating-point rounding when comparing |F|^{1/n}.
- Confusing this with odd-sunflowers (FPP2024) or with k-sunflowers for k > 3.
- Off-by-one in the threshold: compute ceil(1.5545^n + tiny) with exact integers, do not hand-copy.
- Comparing against 1.551 or the unpublished 1.554: on 2026-10-18 the 49a page lists unverified bounds up to 1.5666969 (Cla2026), and by the criterion's rule a listed bound above 1.5545 becomes the bar.
