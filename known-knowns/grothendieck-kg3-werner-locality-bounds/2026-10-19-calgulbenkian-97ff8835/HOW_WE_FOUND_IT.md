# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

The 120 x 120 matrix is the supplementary file the paper's authors published for their E8 instance; the paper prints the resulting ratio 165/109 as a heuristic value because the authors could not solve the underlying sign (binary quadratic) problem. An AI search agent (Gemini, working for the contributor) noticed that the matrix is 3G - 13I for the Gram matrix G of the 120 positive roots of E8, and that the symmetry group of E8 lets one reduce the 2^120 sign choices to a finite list of 18454 representatives. For each of them a linear-programming dual bound was found in floating point and then checked exactly in integers; none exceeds 6104, and two reach it. A lower-bound sign vector reaches 6104 as well, so the maximum is exactly 6104. The scripts that made the certificates are in `verify-it-yourself/optional/`; the command lines of the original run are not recorded.
