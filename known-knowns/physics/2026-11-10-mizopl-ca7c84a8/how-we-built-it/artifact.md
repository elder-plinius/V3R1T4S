# How We Built It — Algebraic & Group-Theoretic Architecture (`magic-state-distillation-fewest-qubits`)

## 1. Exact $\mathbb{F}_2$-Linear Fredholm Alternative Reduction (`Master Theorem 1`)

In the polynomial-algebra framework of Jacinto, Valcarce, and Sangouard (`arXiv:2606.07734v2`), a $T$-to-$T$ magic-state distillation protocol on $N = r + 1$ qubits ($k = 1$ logical row $u \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$, $r$ check rows $X = [x_1, \dots, x_n] \in \mathbb{F}_2^{r \times n}$) with distance $d \ge 4$ satisfies Eqs. (18a)–(18d) and Eq. (19) over $\mathbb{F}_2$. Since deleting zero columns and pairs of identical columns cannot increase the number of qubits $N$ or decrease the fault distance $d \ge 3$, any minimal witness has distinct nonzero check columns $X \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^r \setminus \{0\}$ whose even completion $Y = X$ ($n$ even) or $Y = X \cup \{0\}$ ($n$ odd) satisfies $\mathbf{1}_Y \in \mathrm{RM}(r-3, r)^\perp = \mathrm{RM}(r-4, r)$.

Crucially, on any fixed support $X \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^r \setminus \{0\}$, the distance-$4$ condition Eq. (19) requires that no collinear triple $\{a, b, a \oplus b\} \subseteq X$ has odd logical sum $u(a) \oplus u(b) \oplus u(a \oplus b) = 1$. Over $\mathbb{F}_2$, this is **100% linear** in $u$:
$$u(a) \oplus u(b) \oplus u(a \oplus b) = 0 \pmod 2 \quad \forall \{a, b, a \oplus b\} \subseteq X.$$
Combined with the degree-1 and degree-2 orthogonality equations $\sum_{x \in X} u(x) x_\alpha = 0$ and $\sum_{x \in X} u(x) x_\alpha x_\beta = 0$, the existence of a valid logical row $u \in \mathbb{F}_2^X$ with $\sum_{x \in X} u(x) = 1 \pmod 2$ is decided in microseconds by the **$\mathbb{F}_2$-linear Fredholm Alternative**: a solution exists if and only if the all-ones vector $\mathbf{1}_X$ does **not** lie in the $\mathbb{F}_2$-rowspace spanned by the $r + \binom{r}{2}$ degree-$\le 2$ evaluation masks and the collinear-triple masks $e_a \oplus e_b \oplus e_{a \oplus b}$.
