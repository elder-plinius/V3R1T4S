# Pre-registration v4 — the frozen model and the confirmatory protocol

Written 2026-10-23T23:12:55Z. **No model of mine has been run on any confirmatory split** (seed 42 or seed 20261023).
Every number in this file is from development data: the 82 vault drugs removed, development split seed 7.
`PREREG.md`, `PREREG.v2.md` and `PREREG.v3.md` all stand; this adds the frozen model.

## 1. The frozen model: PotencyTransfer

A drevalpy 1.3.5 model class (`attempts/potency-transfer/code/potency_transfer_model.py`) wrapping the reference implementation
`attempts/potency-transfer/code/potency_core.py`. For an unseen drug d and cell line c it predicts

    cell_line_mean(c) + b + kappa * (delta_hat(d) - mean training delta)

where `cell_line_mean(c)` is exactly what the NaiveMeanEffectsPredictor predicts for an unseen drug, and
`delta_hat` is kernel ridge regression of each training drug's mean residual on
K = Tanimoto(Morgan) + beta * Jaccard(target pathways) + tau * z z^T, z being an NCI-60-trained,
structure-only potency prior. alpha in {0.03,0.1,0.3,1,3}, beta in {0,0.5,1,2}, tau in {0,0.5,1,2,4}, the
intercept b and the shrinkage kappa (clipped to [0,1.5]) are all chosen by 5-fold drug-grouped CV inside
the training drugs, inner seed 12345. Views are identifiers only, so drevalpy evaluates **every drug and
every cell line** — no feature-availability subsetting.

**The NCI-60 teacher** predicts panel-mean log10 GI50 at the standard 10^-4 M top dose from Morgan
fingerprints. Under the pre-committed primary rule (`attempts/potency-transfer/H10-PRECOMMIT.md`, amendment 2) every NCI-60
compound whose salt-stripped parent shares a skeleton (InChIKey block 1) with any CTRPv2 drug, or has
Tanimoto >= 0.60 to any CTRPv2 compound, was removed: 474 compounds, leaving 53,486. **No CTRPv2 drug,
vault drugs included, and no close analogue of one, was in the teacher's training data.** The teacher's
architecture and training schedule were fixed once and never tuned on CTRPv2.

## 2. What development showed, and how much was tried

Development, all logged under `attempts/potency-transfer/output/`: 6 model variants (`dev_variants.log`), 4 teacher exclusion
thresholds (`dev_teacher_thresholds.log`), 3 teacher-weight grids (`dev_tau_grid.log`), plus v1/v2
exploration — about **15 configurations**, each across 10 development splits. The frozen model is variant D
at threshold 0.60 with the original grid. Its development result: mean normalized R^2 **+0.163** (SE 0.039),
positive in 9/10 splits; single agents +0.129; drug-level potency correlation on novel scaffolds 0.45.
These development numbers are **not** the claim.

## 3. Confirmatory runs

drevalpy 1.3.5, October-2025 data (PREREG.v3), CTRPv2, LDO, 10 splits, RMSE tuning, no transformation.

1. **Seed 42**: PotencyTransfer run into the same result folder as the baseline reproduction
   (`repro_seed42`), so it uses the identical saved splits as NaiveMeanEffectsPredictor,
   MultiOmicsNeuralNetwork, GradientBoosting and SimpleNeuralNetwork.
2. **Seed 20261023**: the same, into `repro_seed20261023`, whose splits were generated by drevalpy's own
   `split_dataset` with the pipeline's parameters and loaded by the unmodified pipeline.
3. The statistic is drevalpy's own normalized R^2 from the written predictions. The success conditions are
   those of `PREREG.md` section 3, unchanged.

## 4. Null test (PREREG.md section 6), made concrete

400 random permutations of the 545-drug universe. In each, every drug's fingerprint, pathway set and
teacher value are replaced by those of another drug, consistently in training and test, so structure no
longer belongs to the drug whose responses it is paired with. Seed-42 splits as saved by the pipeline,
training on train+validation as the pipeline does for its final model, same normalization. Run with the
verified-equivalent fast core. The real seed-42 value must exceed **every** control.

## 5. Diagnostics, reported whatever they show (none is the claim)

- normalized R^2 on **single agents only** (drug combinations are an open LDO leak, drevalpy issue #300),
  with the baselines scored on the same single agents;
- drug-level potency correlation by maximum Tanimoto to the training drugs (<0.30, 0.30-0.45, 0.45-0.60, >=0.60);
- normalized R^2 on the **vault drugs only**, both all 82 and the 74 never exposed (`data/VAULT-INCIDENT.md`);
- the same model with the **0.50** teacher (strictest analogue exclusion), on seed 42;
- normalized Pearson and Spearman against partial win p1;
- the top-three ordering of the reproduced baselines across both split generations (partial win p3).

## 6. What would make me withdraw rather than claim

Any condition of `PREREG.md` section 3 failing on seed 42 or on seed 20261023; the null test failing;
the reproduced MultiOmicsNeuralNetwork beating PotencyTransfer on either generation; or a leak found in
the teacher. A pass on one generation and a miss on the other is a miss.
