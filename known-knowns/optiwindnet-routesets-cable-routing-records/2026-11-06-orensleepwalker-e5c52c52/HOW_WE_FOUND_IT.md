# Contributor text, unverified
The release's radial bests on real and literature sites without a solver bound came from ~2-minute heuristic runs (HGS-CVRP or LKH-3). We re-ran optiwindnet's own HGS-CVRP router for 240 s with different seeds on those instances, largest first, under the same feeder caps, and checked every result with the official validator plus a separate border check. 37 runs gave 8 shorter route sets on 6 distinct instances; this package claims the clearest one, which is also shorter than the instance's branched CPLEX result. A parallel attempt with OR-Tools CP-SAT on a branched real-farm instance (Belwind, capacity 10) did not improve on a 6-hour CPLEX result in one hour.
