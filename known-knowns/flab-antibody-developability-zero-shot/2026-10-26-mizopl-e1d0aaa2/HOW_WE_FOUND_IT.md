## 1. Diagnosing Why Deep Protein Language Models Flip Sign on FLAb (`p3`)

When we audited the 12 released models in `Graylab/FLAb` (`score/`), we observed two striking anomalies:
1. On **Polyreactivity (`score/polyreactivity`, 6 datasets)**, every sequence protein language model (`antiberty`, `iglm`, `progen/small`, `progen/medium`, `progen/base`, `progen/large`, `progen/BFD90`, `progen/oas`, `progen/xlarge`) has a **negative** category mean Spearman under standard model confidence (`-perplexity`, from `-0.0162` down to `-0.1936`), whereas inverse-folding models (`mpnn = +0.1906`, `esmif = +0.1026`) and PyRosetta (`+0.1123`) are positive.
2. Why do sequence PLMs predict that higher-likelihood antibodies are *more* polyreactive (worse)? By computing exact human IMGT V+J germline sequence identity (`pure_germline_identity`) and residualizing all models against sequence length $L$ and germline identity $G$ (`p3`), we found that human germline identity $G$ correlates positively with thermostability (`+0.1297` on `score/tm`), but **negatively** with favourable low polyreactivity (`-0.2129` on `score/polyreactivity`, including `-0.6983` on `Rosace2023_CR3022_CIC` and `-0.7379` on `Rosace2023_Golimumab_CIC`). Several human germline VH1/VH3/VH4 genes carry cationic and aromatic CDR patches that stabilize the native fold yet promote non-specific stickiness in CIC/PSR/ELISA assays. Because sequence PLMs act primarily as germline-proximity detectors, their raw likelihoods flip sign between Thermostability and Polyreactivity.

## 2. Diagnosing Simpson's Paradox in Unweighted Category Means (`n = 2` vs `n = 137`)

In our initial whole-chain exploratory composite (`CLSS_EqWt6_IMGT_Z`, pre-registered at `2026-10-25T23:43:00Z` in `L-FADZ-004`, which standardizes the 6 intensive sequence-level descriptors against the external 432-pair human IMGT germline reference repertoire and sums them with equal weights `1/6`), inspecting `Wittrup2017_CST_Tm` ($N = 137$ clinical-stage antibodies across diverse human V-gene families in `score/tm`) revealed that whole-chain `CLSS_EqWt6_IMGT_Z` had `-0.0028` on `Wittrup2017_CST_Tm` despite `+0.3528` unweighted mean!
Why? Because in `score/tm` (15 datasets), 3 datasets have $n = 2$ antibodies (`Hie2022_C143_Tm`, `Hie2022_MEDI8852_Tm`, `Hie2022_mAb114UCA_Tm`) and 5 more have $n = 5\text{--}8$. Under unweighted averaging, whole-chain `bp_flexibility` appears strongly positive (`+0.2908` unweighted) even though on the 137 clinical antibodies in `Wittrup2017_CST_Tm` it is **negative (`-0.1454`)**!

## 3. Defining and Freezing `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` (`FWR1–4` vs `CDR1–3`) & Evaluating All Categories Including `Binding`

An antibody variable domain is structurally divided into a conserved $\beta$-sandwich **Framework scaffold (`FWR1–4`)** (which governs cooperative thermal unfolding $T_m$) and three hypervariable **CDR loops (`CDR1–3`)** (which govern antigen binding and paratope surface stickiness/self-association).
We defined **`CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z`** (`2026-10-26T00:13:00Z`, frozen at `2026-10-26T00:26:00Z` in commit `654c23f056b932c310723f591bc3fec89102ad73`) by taking **all 7** published Biopython `ProtParam` descriptors from `FLAb/models/biopython_score.py` and assigning each to its physical region via the four conserved immunoglobulin landmarks (`Cys22`, `Trp36`, `Cys92/104`, `WGQG/FGQG`):
- **Scaffold folding stability descriptors** (`fwr_flex`, `neg_fwr_gravy`, `neg_fwr_inst`) on **`FWR1–4`**,
- **Paratope stickiness/electrostatic descriptors** (`neg_cdr_arom`, `neg_cdr_pI`, `neg_cdr_chg`) on **`CDR1–3`** (matching the 3 CDR surface properties of Raybould et al., *PNAS* 2019 Therapeutic Antibody Profiler), and
- **Whole-chain descriptors** (`mw` and `germ`),
standardized exclusively against the external 432-pair human IMGT germline reference repertoire with strictly equal `1/8` weights.

Because `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` was defined at `00:13Z` after looking at per-dataset results on `Wittrup2017_CST_Tm` in `score/tm` (and its predecessor `CLSS_EqWt6_IMGT_Z` had been scored on `score/polyreactivity` at `23:51Z`), Thermostability (`p1` and main category claim) and Polyreactivity are **not claimed** as a Full Win. Furthermore, when the frozen `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` rule (unchanged from commit `654c23f0`) is evaluated across all `16` `score/binding` datasets (`6,959` rows) and all `80` `data/binding` CSVs (`97,778` rows), it achieves **`-0.0060`** (`+0.0060` for $+K_d$) on `score/binding` vs `iglm = 0.1742` (`margin = -0.1802`), **`-0.0987`** (`36/80 > 0`) across all `80` `data/binding` CSVs, **`-0.1123`** (`5/12 > 0`) across the `12` primary `score/`-mapped `data/binding` CSVs, and **`-0.1640`** (`5/15 > 0`) across the `15` study-mapped `data/binding` CSVs (because antigen-specific binding affinity depends on target-epitope complementarity rather than antigen-agnostic intrinsic biophysics). Accordingly, **Binding, Full Win, and `p1` are not claimed**, and this submission claims **Partial Wins `p2` and `p3`** (`status: CONFIRMED_PARTIAL`).
