## 2. Why `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` Has Zero Degrees of Freedom Tuned on `Polyreactivity` or the Untouched Categories

1. **Complete Descriptor Inclusion (`8/8`, Weight `1/8` Each):**
   - `Graylab/FLAb` ships exactly 7 sequence-based Biopython `ProtParam` descriptors in `models/biopython_score.py` (`bp_average_flexibility`, `bp_gravy`, `bp_instability_index`, `bp_aromaticity`, `bp_isoelectric_point`, `bp_charge_at_7_4`, `bp_molecular_weight`) plus germline distance (`models/ld_score.py`).
   - `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` includes **all 7** Biopython descriptors plus `germ` with equal weight `1/8` (no descriptor subset selection).
2. **Structural Partitioning Determined by Immunoglobulin Anatomy (`FWR1–4` vs `CDR1–3`):**
   - Each variable domain is split at the 4 invariant vertebrate immunoglobulin landmarks (`Cys22`, `Trp36`, `Cys92/104`, `WGQG/FGQG`) into the $\beta$-sandwich scaffold (`FWR1–4`: conformational flexibility `fwr_flex`, hydropathy `neg_fwr_gravy`, dipeptide stability `neg_fwr_inst`), the hypervariable binding loops (`CDR1–3`: aromaticity `neg_cdr_arom`, isoelectric point `neg_cdr_pI`, net charge `neg_cdr_chg`, matching the 3 CDR surface properties of Raybould et al., *PNAS* 2019 Therapeutic Antibody Profiler), and the whole chain (`mw`, `germ`).
3. **Evaluation on Categories and Datasets Never Scored Prior to Commit `654c23f056b932c310723f591bc3fec89102ad73`:**
