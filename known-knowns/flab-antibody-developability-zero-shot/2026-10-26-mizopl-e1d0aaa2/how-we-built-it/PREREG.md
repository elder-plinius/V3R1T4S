## 2. Summary of Category Eligibility & Why Only Partial Wins `p2` and `p3` Are Claimed

1. **Thermostability (`score/tm` 15 datasets, `data/thermostability` 20 & 21 datasets, and the `score/tm` plus `data/thermostability` 25-dataset union) — `p1` and Full Win NOT Claimed:**
   - Although `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` achieves **`+0.4636`** (`14/15 = 93.3% > 0`) on `score/tm`, **`+0.3270`** (`16/20 = 80.0% > 0`) on `data_thermostability_20_standard`, **`+0.3048`** (`16/21 = 76.2% > 0`) on `data_thermostability_21_all`, and **`+0.2834`** (`19/25 = 76.0% > 0`) on the `score/tm` plus `data/thermostability` 25-dataset union, `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` was defined at `00:13Z` after looking at per-dataset results on `Wittrup2017_CST_Tm` in `score/tm`. Therefore, Thermostability (`p1` and main category claim) is **not claimed**.
2. **Polyreactivity (`score/polyreactivity` 6 datasets, `data/polyreactivity` 25 datasets, and 26-dataset union) — Full Win NOT Claimed:**
   - Its predecessor `CLSS_EqWt6_IMGT_Z` had been scored on `score/polyreactivity` at `23:51Z`, before the `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` rule was chosen (`+0.4467` on `score/polyreactivity` [`6/6 = 100.0% > 0`], `+0.1407` [`17/25 > 0`] on `data/polyreactivity`, `+0.1671` [`18/26 > 0`] on the 26-dataset union).
3. **Aggregation, Expression, and Immunogenicity (`score/`) — NOT Claimed:**
   - On `score/aggregation` (`+0.0997` vs `progen/oas (+ppl) = +0.1701`), `score/expression` (`+0.1205` vs `progen/medium = +0.3159`), and `score/immunogenicity` (`-0.2584` vs `iglm (+ppl) = +0.0890`), `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` is `0.0704`, `0.1954`, and `0.3474` below the best released method. (On the untouched `data/expression/` 4-CSV universe never loaded prior to commit `654c23f0`, `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` achieves `+0.1913` [`4/4 = 100.0% > 0`].)
4. **Pharmacokinetics (`data/pharmacokinetics`, 9 datasets) — NOT Claimed as Full Win:**
   - `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` achieves `+0.2287` (`6/9 = 66.7% > 0`), but `score/` has no pharmacokinetics files, so there is no released method in `score/` to beat.
5. **Binding (`score/binding` 16 datasets and `data/binding` 80 CSVs) — NOT Claimed:**
   - Running the frozen `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` rule from commit `654c23f0`, unchanged, across `score/binding` (`16` datasets, `6,959` rows) and all `80` `data/binding` CSVs (`97,778` rows) yields **`-0.0060`** (`6/16 = 37.5% > 0` for $-K_d$, or **`+0.0060`** [`10/16 = 62.5% > 0`] for $+K_d$) on `score/binding` vs best released `iglm = 0.1742` (`margin = -0.1802` for $-K_d$, `-0.1682` for $+K_d`), **`-0.0987`** (`36/80 = 45.0% > 0`; `-0.1012` [`35/77 > 0`] on the 77 standard-header CSVs) across all `80` `data/binding` CSVs, **`-0.1123`** (`5/12 = 41.7% > 0`) on the `12` primary `score/`-mapped `data/binding` CSVs (`-0.1640` [`5/15 = 33.3% > 0`] on the `15` study-mapped CSVs), **`-0.0964`** (`31/68 = 45.6% > 0`) on the `68` excluded `data/binding` CSVs (`-0.0837` [`31/65 = 47.7% > 0`] on the `65` excluded CSVs), and **`-0.0809`** (`37/84 = 44.0% > 0`) on the `84`-dataset `score/binding` plus `data/binding` union. Because `CLSS_Reg8_AllBP_IMGT_Z` does not beat every released method's mean on `score/binding` by at least `0.05`, **Binding is not claimed**.
6. **Claimed Partial Wins (`p2` and `p3`):**
   - **`p2` (Positively correlated on $\ge 80\%$ of datasets in a category):** `14/15 = 93.3%` on `score/tm`, `16/20 = 80.0%` on standard `data/thermostability`, `6/6 = 100.0%` on `score/polyreactivity`, and `4/4 = 100.0%` on untouched `data/expression`.
   - **`p3` (Reproducible length & germline confound control):** Full rank-residualized partial Spearman ($\perp L$, $\perp G$, $\perp (L,G)$) and exact matched-stratum Spearman ($\bar{\rho}_{\text{matched}}$) across all 12 released models, 9 single descriptors, and 10 CLSS parameter-free methods (`+0.4308` matched-stratum on `score/tm` and `+0.5262` on `score/polyreactivity`).
