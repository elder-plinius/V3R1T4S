# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

**Brief.** Physlib defines the real Clifford algebra of `diracForm` (signature (+,−,−,−) on ℝ⁴), the real
subalgebra `diracAlgebra` of 4×4 complex matrices generated by the Dirac matrices, and the algebra map
`ofCliffordAlgebra` between them; it proves the map surjective and leaves "injectivity (requires dimension theory)"
and the isomorphism as a TODO. The status page was fetched once at 2026-10-14T06:16:28Z (HTTP 200). The statement
was frozen in `how-we-built-it/PREREG.md` before any attempt.

**What worked.** A dimension count (lens "kernel-first"), first Lean call at 06:25:55 UTC and a complete proof at
06:31 UTC; the checker passed at 06:34 UTC.
1. dim_ℝ Cl = 16: Mathlib's basis of the exterior algebra indexed by `Finset (Fin 4)` transported along
   `CliffordAlgebra.equivExterior` (2 is invertible in ℝ).
2. dim_ℝ diracAlgebra ≥ 16: the 16 ordered products of gamma matrices are ℝ-linearly independent. The proof
   conjugates to Physlib's chiral basis, where the products are monomial matrices with entries in {0, ±1, ±i}, and
   reads off real and imaginary parts of eight entries (`linarith`).
3. Physlib's surjectivity gives ≤ 16, so the dimensions agree, and a surjective linear map between spaces of equal
   finite dimension is injective.
An exact-arithmetic Python check (`how-we-built-it/out/numcheck.txt`) confirmed the rank 16 beforehand. A second
lens ("construct-space") reached its own proof; it is not part of this package.

**What failed or was not needed.** The exterior-basis route first failed with "The environment does not contain
Module.Basis.ExteriorAlgebra": Physlib does not import that Mathlib file, so the object imports it (the checker's
import policy allows Mathlib). Spanning arguments by Clifford induction, trace orthogonality, a decidable
Gaussian-integer surrogate, complexification and the simplicity of Cl(1,3) ≅ M₂(ℍ) were planned but not needed.
