## 1. Auditing `arXiv:2606.29183v2` and the Automorphism-Group Literature (`p2`)
When scouting Yawara Ishida's July 2026 preprint (*"No involutions in the missing Moore graph"*, `arXiv:2606.29183v2`), we noticed two key structural features:
1. The paper is a pure-mathematics manuscript with no accompanying computational artifact, yet every step of its proof — from the $p$-integrality of the Bose–Mesner idempotents $E_7 = \frac{400I + 50A - J}{750}$ and $E_{-8} = \frac{455I - 65A + J}{975}$ to the $\mathbb{F}_p$-ranks of their principal submatrices $E_\theta[\mathrm{Fix}(x)]$ on fixed-point subgraphs — reduces to exact rational and finite-field linear algebra.
2. While `arXiv:2606.29183v2` proves Theorem 1.1 ($2 \nmid |\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma)|$) and Theorem 1.2 (exact displacement counts $a_1(x)$ for odd primes $p \in \{7, 11, 13, 19\}$), it does **not** propagate those prime-order restrictions — or generalize the Brauer-quotient trace–rank identity to composite orders $p \cdot q$ — into the candidate odd-order automorphism groups classified by Makhnev & Paduchikh (*Proc. Steklov Inst. Math.* 267 (2009), S149–S163, Theorem 1) and Kováčiková (`arXiv:1812.05353`, p. 39).

## 2. Discovering the Unconditional Exclusions of $|\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma)| = 275$ and $|\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma)| = 175$ (`p1`, Part I)
Inspecting Theorem 1(2)(iii)–(iv) and Lemmas 4.4–4.5 of Makhnev & Paduchikh (2009), we examined the two largest non-$p$-group abelian candidates left open: $G \cong C_{11} \times C_5^2$ ($|G| = 275$) and $G \cong C_7 \times C_5^2$ ($|G| = 175$).
- For $|G| = 275$ ($G = \langle x \rangle \times U$, $|x| = 11, |U| = 25$), $\mathrm{Fix}(x) \cong C_5$ ($5$ vertices) is fixed pointwise by $U$. Could any $u \in U \setminus \{1\}$ have $\mathrm{Fix}(u) \cong \mathrm{HoSi} = \mathrm{srg}(50, 7, 0, 1)$? If so, $x$ of order $11$ would act on $\mathrm{HoSi}$ with $50 \equiv 6 \pmod{11}$ fixed vertices; but since $\mathrm{HoSi}$ has degree $7 < 11$, no $x$-fixed vertex can have a neighbor in an $11$-cycle of $x$, forcing $\mathrm{Fix}_{\mathrm{HoSi}}(x)$ to be a union of connected components of $\mathrm{HoSi}$ — impossible! Thus $\mathrm{Fix}(g) = C_5$ for **every** $g \in G \setminus \{1\}$, so $G$ acts freely on $V(\Gamma) \setminus C_5$ ($3245$ vertices), which immediately fails $275 \mid 3245$ ($3245 \equiv 220 \pmod{275}$), and independently forces $a_1(x^k) \ge 550 > 500$ (violating both Mačaj–Širáň Lemma 12 and the Abelian Orbit Chord Lemma).
- For $|G| = 175$ ($G = \langle x \rangle \times U$, $|x| = 7, |U| = 25$), $\Omega = \mathrm{Fix}(U) \cong \mathrm{HoSi}$ ($50$ vertices), and $U$ acts freely on the $a_0(x)-1$ leaves of the star $\mathrm{Fix}(x) \cong K_{1, a_0(x)-1}$ outside $\Omega$, uniquely forcing $a_0(x) = 51$. Then $\mathrm{Fix}(x)$ ($51$) and the non-fixed leaf neighborhoods ($50 \times 56 = 2800$) consume $2851$ vertices, leaving at most $399$ vertices for $S_1(x) \sqcup S_1(x^2) \sqcup S_1(x^3)$. However, inside $\Omega \cong \mathrm{HoSi}$ we have $a_1^\Omega(x^k) = 7$, so $a_1(x^k) \equiv 7 \pmod{175}$ and $a_1(x^k) \equiv 2 \pmod{15} \implies a_1(x^k) \ge 182$ for $k = 1, 2, 3$, requiring $2851 + 3 \times 182 = 3397 > 3250$ vertices!

## 3. Discovering the Equivariant Galois–Brauer Rank Obstruction for Composite Orders $p \cdot q$ (`p1`, Part II)
To attack the composite cyclic groups $C_{55}, C_{65}, C_{35}$ left open in Makhnev & Paduchikh (2009), we extended Ishida's Theorem 4.1 from a single prime-order automorphism $x$ ($|x| = p$) to a pair of commuting automorphisms $(x, f)$ of distinct prime orders $p \ne q$ by working over the unramified cyclotomic DVR $\mathcal{O} = \mathbb{Z}_p[\zeta_q]$ and applying the Brauer quotient to the $q$ orthogonal twisted idempotents $E_\theta \Pi_r$ ($\Pi_r = \frac{1}{q}\sum_{j=0}^{q-1} \zeta_q^{-rj} T_f^j$). Because $\mathrm{tr}(T_{xf^j} E_\theta) \in \mathbb{Q}$ and $\hat{c}_r = \mathrm{rank}_\kappa(E_\theta[F]\Pi_r[F] \bmod p) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$, invariance under $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_q)/\mathbb{Q})$ forces $\hat{c}_1 = \dots = \hat{c}_{q-1}$, yielding the divisibility obstruction $(q-1) \mid (\mathrm{rank}_{\mathbb{F}_p}(E_\theta[F]) - \hat{c}_0)$. Applying this at $(p, q) = (11, 5)$ immediately kills Case A ($\mathrm{Fix}(f) = \emptyset$) of $C_{55}$ ($4 \nmid (2 - 0)$) and uniquely pins down the displacement spectra of Case B of $C_{55}$, $C_{65}$, and $C_{35}$.
