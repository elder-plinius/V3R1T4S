# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

This note summarizes our diagnostic and operator-design workflow (`contributor text, unverified`). The claim itself rests solely on the frozen code and JSON certificates in `object/` and the standalone verifier in `verify-it-yourself/verify.py`.

1. **Diagnosing the Three Coupled Failure Modes on `4` Dev Datasets:** Dissecting `42_slide_tag_human_skin_melanoma`, `44_stereo_seq_Drosophila_embryo_E7`, `34_MERFISH_Human_cortex`, and `01_10x_Visium_mouse_brain` showed that existing SVG methods split along three coupled confounders (`object/ablation_p3.json`): (i) dividing raw counts by spot library size $L_s = \sum_g Y_{s,g}$ injects spurious spatial autocorrelation into `alpha = 0` null genes (`MoranI(log1p(L_s)) = 0.293064` on `42_melanoma`) and cancels compartment-aligned SVGs (`mean drop = -0.154900` when injected into `AMS-SVG`); (ii) unpruned Delaunay triangulations bridge empty tissue cavities on irregular point clouds (`p99 / median = 3.9565x` on melanoma) while raw count inner products (`SPARK-X`) are dominated by Negative Binomial tail bursts; and (iii) mapping test statistics through IEEE-754 float64 `-log10(max(p, 1e-300))` ties up to `81.6%` of genes at `Z >= 37.17` on high-SNR slices.
2. **Freezing `object/method.py` Before the Single `46`-Dataset Holdout Run:** Combining gene-marginal `log1p` + Freeman-Tukey variance stabilization, three locally density-adapted biweight kNN operators (`k in {8, 20, 48}`), three `O(2)`-invariant low-rank Mercer polynomial-Gaussian operators, and exact Cliff & Ord kurtosis-adjusted permutation $Z$-scores resolved all three confounders in pure `O(N k G)` CPU matrix multiplications (`0.945981` `50`-dataset mean, `0.953362` `46`-holdout mean).
3. **Finite-UMI Ceiling, Two Operator Traps & Capture-Dropout Extension (`Wave 2`):** Computing an offline 20-column leave-one-`alpha`-out template ceiling (`0.835238` on `42_melanoma`) showed most remaining gap to `1.0` comes from finite-UMI NB shot noise across `Delta_alpha = 0.05` steps. Testing `R^{-1}` scale whitening exposed Kac-Murdock-Szego boundary concentration (`0.797905 -> 0.780190`), and per-column NB dispersion estimation exposed `scDesign3`'s `1 - 2*alpha*(1 - alpha)` variance-dip curvature shift (`0.797905 -> 0.776000`). Replacing equal-weight scale averaging with Hilbert-sphere geodesic arc-length weights (`arccos(R_{s,s+1})`) and adding a scale-separated Wiener + deflated-spectral Empirical-Bayes high-pass capture gate (`object/ams_svg_eb_v2.py`) won all `5` regimes of the 9-dataset Depth-Paradox benchmark while clarifying single-spot anatomical ribbon collinearity (`-0.019809` on `11_Cerebellum`).
