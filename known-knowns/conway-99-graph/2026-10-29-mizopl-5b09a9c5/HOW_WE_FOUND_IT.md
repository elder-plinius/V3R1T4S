1. **Anchoring the Forced-Structure Reduction at a Fixed Vertex $x$:**
   In Section 2.3 of `arXiv:2608.11211`, Aalok Thakkar & Simone Severini (2026) — building on the neighborhood-anchored prime-automorphism framework of Patrick G. Cesarz & Andrew J. 2. **Galois-Spectral Trace Theorem & Orbit-Valency Projection ($p = 7, 5$):**

Projecting the quadratic orbit equation onto the top $6 \times 12$ submatrix $[M \mid Q]$ over $I_{LL} \cup I_{RR}$ with $S_3 \times S_3 \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_2 \times S_6$ symmetry breaking, column upper bounds (`L_c_up <= 6`, `L_c_diag <= 5`), and the duplicate-free row difference identity (`L_diff == 18`) collapses the $> 48\text{-hour}$ CP-SAT timeout of `arXiv:2608.11211` into a $< 1.4\text{-second}$ pure-RUP refutation.
3. **Involution Spectral Trace, Flipped-Edge Window & Global Flipped-Edge Triangle Bijection Theorem ($p = 2$):**
