## p1 quoted in full (the claim record)
"New excluded family: a rigorous non-existence proof for an explicit infinite family not covered by arXiv 2604.28072's 1,072 fibers (e.g. fibers with parameter >= 100, or a whole elliptic fibration of the de Grey-Gibbs-Helm reformulation), with every rank/descent computation rerunnable by the verifier in PARI or Magma and the written argument checked line by line (Lean preferred)."

## Clause-by-clause
1. Explicit infinite family: MET. Two families in (p,q), with e1, e2, cSq as explicit polynomials (object/dgh_internal_edge_cuboids_proof.lean, Section 2). I compared them term by term with arXiv:2401.06784 Section 7.4 (Theorem 5 and following text): integer edges, perpendicular face diagonal, other integer edge and the squared third edge all match for both generators (x = 4p^2q^2 - p^4 + q^4 and x = (p^2+q^2)(p-q)^2 - 4p^2q^2). Section 10.2 of that paper does pose "members of the two internal-ratio families can never be PCs" as open. Infinitude of positive edge cuboids is proved in Lean (..._infinite_positive_edge_cuboids, k+1,k and 2k,1).
2. "Rigorous non-existence proof": MET for the stated statement. The Lean file has no imports, 1644 lines; grep finds no sorry, native_decide, axiom, unsafe, extern or implemented_by. It compiles with the named toolchain (lean v4.35.0 from ~/.elan, 15 s) and prints 12 #print axioms blocks, every one [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], including dghInternalFamilyA_cSq_not_square and dghInternalFamilyB_cSq_not_square. The proof route (read in full for the chain): cSq(p,q) equals the unified cSq(a,e)=e^2(e^4-a^2e^2-a^4)(e^4+3a^2e^2+3a^4) at (a,e)=(p^2-q^2,2pq) or (2pq,p^2-q^2) (ring identity, by grind); reduce a primitive Pythagorean triple, remove the common factor, turn "rational square" into the coprime-factor condition on (g^2,a^2), and finish by Euler's x^4+x^2y^2+y^4=z^2 descent (no_euler_quartic_pos). I re-derived the key factor identities by hand (a^4+2a^2e^2+e^4-3a^2(a^2+e^2)+a^4 = e^4-a^2e^2-a^4, and the second factor 3a^4+3a^2e^2+e^4) and they agree. Independent numeric spot check (not a proof): over 1<=p,q<150, 44104 evaluations of both cSq families gave 0 integer squares.
3. "Every rank/descent computation rerunnable in PARI or Magma": the proof contains no rank, Selmer or descent COMPUTATION outside Lean. Main theorems A and B depend on: natural/integer arithmetic lemmas, Nat.gcd/coprime lemmas, euler_quartic_descent_step, no_coprime_euler_quartic, no_euler_quartic_pos, int_sq_of_rat_sq, dgh_quartic_to_euler_sum_sq, no_dgh_unified_square, all inside the Lean file; the axiom audit confirms nothing is trusted from outside the kernel (no reduceBool). The four 15a8/15a3 lemmas (bridge, mod5, mod16 obstructions) are not used by A or B; I checked that the proofs of A and B call only no_dgh_unified_square. I judge the clause met vacuously and, more to the point, replaced by something stronger: the verifier reruns the argument in Lean, and "every" ranges over an empty set. Wording caveat: the README sentence "uses no rank or descent computation" is true for computations but the proof is itself a Fermat descent (formalized); a reader could be misled by the unqualified word.
4. "Written argument checked line by line (Lean preferred)": MET, Lean used and compiled by me. Theorems C and D (scaled perfect cuboid) carry an unused IsPerfectCuboid hypothesis; the README says so honestly and rests the claim on A and B.
5. "Not covered by the 1,072 fibers": plausible, NOT demonstrated by the package. The package never proves a map between its (p,q) families and Peschmann's master-tuple (m,n) fibers; "disjoint" is argued only by parameter size (p,q >= 100 witnesses). I did not fetch Peschmann's paper (the brief allowed one fetch, used for DGH). The families are infinite with unbounded parameters, so a finite set of bounded fibers cannot be their whole content; whether an individual member already lies on an excluded fiber is a novelty question, outside this review.

## README link: "cSq never a square" -> "no perfect cuboid in the families"
Correct for the two families as DGH define them. In each family the member is an edge cuboid (e1, e2 integer, c = sqrt(cSq)). Any rescaling of it by lam/mu being a perfect cuboid needs c rational, so cSq (an integer) would be a rational, hence integer, square, which the theorem forbids. int_sq_of_rat_sq makes the rescaling step exact. Both polynomials are homogeneous in (p,q), so integer p,q lose no generality over rational ratios; the theorem even covers non-coprime and same-parity (p,q), wider than DGH's coprime opposite-parity setting (fine, stronger). Degenerate p=0, q=0, p^2=q^2 are excluded and give a zero edge anyway.

## README wording versus evidence
- Accurate: Lean results, axiom list, 12 theorems, witnesses, the stated scope.
- Slightly wide: "unconditional ... excluding all 2 open ... fibrations" is fine only as "excluding perfect cuboids in these two families"; the "Not claimed" section correctly says the general problem is open. "disjoint from the 1072 bounded fibers" is asserted, not proved (item 5). The Lean header "Complete Resolution" is rhetorical; the theorem statements are what count.
- The "no rank or descent computation" sentence is accurate only in the sense of item 3.
