# Exclusion of Both Open Internal-Ratio Edge-Cuboid Fibrations of van Luijk and de Grey, Gibbs, and Helm

## What

Machine-checked in Lean 4 (v4.35.0, only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound) that for all nonzero integers p, q with p^2 != q^2 the squared third edge of each of the two de Grey-Gibbs-Helm internal-ratio edge-cuboid families (arXiv:2401.06784 Section 7.4) is never an integer square, hence no member of either family, under any rational rescaling, is a perfect cuboid; the package does not prove that these families avoid Peschmann's 1,072 fibers and uses no PARI or Magma computation.
