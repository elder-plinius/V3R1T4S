# HOW WE FOUND IT (contributor text, unverified)

Same night, same sweep, second cell. The run of 2026-10-16 priced all 135 sparse gap rows of
Packomania's circles-in-a-circle table with twelve lattice windows each (two seconds of
arithmetic) and found exactly two rows where a plain triangular window already beat the posted
record: N = 4141 (submitted first, PR #234) and this one, N = 3307. Both rows turn out to carry
un-refined lattice seeds — the criterion skeptic's structural check found 3292 of the posted
config's 3307 nearest-neighbour distances sitting at one exact minimum, the signature of a raw
lattice rather than an optimized packing.

This cell waited a day for its panel (one cell per claim, one panel per run): a fresh
verification against the re-fetched live table, then three fresh-context skeptics — an
independent 62-digit reproduction, a live-table novelty re-fetch, and a clause-by-clause
criterion audit that also re-fetched the queue and history pages. All three passed; the margin
is 4.4e-6, five orders above the 1e-11 bar.

Nothing else in the sweep's reach is this soft: the other 133 gap rows beat every plain window
by 1e-5 or more. The next nights' leads (boundary-ring relaxation on rows 2611/2773/3901, which
sit only 3-4e-6 above the plain-window family, and strained lattices) are recorded in the
workspace dossier.
