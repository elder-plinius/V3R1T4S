# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

After confirming an evaluator (p2: 63 of 67 A1 events of 1971 recovered on S12 with templates from 1972), 25 nests were
scanned on S14 MH1/MH2 over 1971 with glitch-screened stacks of each nest's own catalogued 1971 events (whitened,
0.25-1.0 Hz, 450 s windows), all fixed in a pre-registration before the scan. Fourteen uncatalogued seeds reached a
single-channel CC of 0.6; the three strongest nests were tested against 401 controls over the full scanned span and
against gap, glitch and thermal checks. One (A38) is a separate package. An earlier candidate of another nest turned out
to start within 0.04 s of a spike in a published acceleration-glitch catalogue, so this claim named that catalogue in
its pre-registration and checked it first: no catalogued glitch lies in this event's window, and the match survives
muting the samples the glitch rule flags and rebuilding the templates on the other decimation grid.
