8. Robustness observation (mine; it does not change the verdict). Both the template and the data window carry 81-85% of their power in 0.75-1.0 Hz. 60.5% of the MH2 NCC comes from that band (50.8% for MH1). So the NCC oscillates with lag at a period of about 1.1-1.2 s. One cycle away, at +1.21 s, D is 0.641. This is the same lag G2 reports, so the onset carries a one-cycle (about 1.2 s) ambiguity. The narrow band also explains the disclosed half-sample test (0.613 / 0.349). The decimated template against data shifted -0.15 s gives MH2 0.349, and against data shifted +0.15 s gives 0.619. This is the carrier, not a glitch. The fair test, with templates rebuilt on the other grid, gives 0.600 / 0.690. The phase-randomised controls keep this narrow amplitude spectrum, so the 0.318 maximum already allows for it.
