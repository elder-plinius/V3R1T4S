# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

The same scan that produced our earlier full-criterion claim (package 2026-11-13-[credit]-9ff1c507, the A100-like
event of 1973-11-26) also produced this event: twelve deep-moonquake nests stacked on Apollo S14 (MH1/MH2) and S16
(MH1/MH2) from their own catalogued 1973 events, all of 1973 scanned with a two-station coincidence statistic that
lets each station take its best lag within 2.1 s, each nest tested against its own 401 controls (time-reversed data,
and S16 circularly shifted by 1-21 h). The 1973-10-30 event was the second-strongest uncatalogued A100 detection that
met the per-channel reading of "NCC >= 0.5 on >= 2 stations"; it was deliberately left out of the first claim.

Before claiming it on its own evidence, a new pre-registration fixed every test before computing it. The null was
recomputed over all of 1973 (the rerun matches the original scan in every science field on every day). The published acceleration-glitch
catalogue of Liu, Xiao and Li (2025), which lists dozens of S14 and S16 entries on these days, has nothing in either
window. Tests that could take the A100 attribution away were run as attacks: all 12 stacks (only A100 is above its
null), the lag profile, the Nakamura 2005 moveouts, S16 MH2's response to the catalogued events that day (it was dead:
a large A1 event 85 minutes earlier shows strongly on MH1 and barely on MH2), and a reverse search with the event's own
waveform over all of 1973, which ranks six catalogued A100 events first to sixth of 998 deep moonquakes. No catalogued
1973 event of another nest or type reaches the A100 null near its start time.

What we could not do away with: this event comes from a scan of many templates, and the station pair S14 + S16 was
chosen after the same event had been a near miss on S12 + S14. A null with 401 controls cannot show a probability
below 1/402 for one template; a family-wise test over the 12 templates of this scan gives 1/402, and a plain
Bonferroni over every two-station template scan of 1973 in our run gives only 0.065. The two 1973 events share the
template, the pipeline and the S16 MH2 explanation, so they do not confirm each other.
