## Hidden quantifiers and selection

- R7 quantifier: "NCC >= 0.5 ... on >= 2 stations" is read as "some channel at each station". This was fixed in advance and is fair for 3-component LP stations with one dead channel. A stricter station-mean or multi-component reading would fail at S16 (0.343), only because S16 MH2 was unresponsive that day: on the same day the reference events have r2 of 0.02-0.13, and in days 301-330 the median r2 is 0.137 (results/fullwin/a3_decision.txt).
- Null quantifier: if "the maximum obtained on a null set" were read across all 12 nest templates searched, it would be 0.625 (A20) and the claim would fail. That reading is not fair: those high control maxima come from shifts that line up real events of other nests, and they calibrate different templates. The per-template null, with a tail probability of about 1e-12 per year, makes the multiplicity irrelevant: 25 nests attempted, 12 stacked, and an unknown number of other lenses in the parent run.
- Lag freedom: each station may move +/- 7 samples (2.113 s), and S16 sits exactly at +7. The +/- 30 s lag scan shows this is the global S16 maximum (next 0.148), not a peak cut off at the edge. The null uses the same freedom.
- Choice among detections: 5 new detections above the A100 null; 3 meet R7, all A100. The claim takes the one with the largest Dt. The second, 1973-10-30 02:25:51.5, also meets R7, R8 and R9 and is not claimed, so this choice does not inflate anything.
- The event is weak: the S14 and S16 energy ratios are about 2.5, around the 80th-85th percentile of random times on the same day. S14 MH1 sits at 511 counts for at least 98% of samples. So the detection rests on correlation, as it should for template matching. The 30-s block NCCs are positive in all 15 blocks on S14 MH2 (0.28-0.86) and on S16 MH1 (0.34-0.81), so the match is spread over the whole window rather than carried by one feature. The time-reversed control (0.157) argues against a stationary tone.
- R9 is close to uninformative for this nest (271.6 deg interval). The phase evidence that actually discriminates is the descriptive 27.2-d cadence, not the pre-registered gate.

## Attribution (is it A100 and not another source?)

- Every other stacked nest is far below (A1 test): Dt 0.087-0.143, all below their own nulls, none with NCC >= 0.5 at either station.
- Reverse search (the candidate's own window scanned over all of 1973): the 6 most similar of 998 catalogued deep moonquakes are all A100. Two of them are not stack members (01-03, rank 4; 08-09, rank 5), which is independent support. The 99th percentile of random windows is 0.138; the top A100 match is 0.446.
- Nests that the moveout cannot tell apart: A35 (5N 36.1E, inside A100's large error ellipse of -2+/-4.2, 32.2+/-17.1) has 10 events in 1973, all scoring <= 0.106 (rank >= 354). A7 has 1 event at 0.105. A244 (far side) has none in 1973.
- Remaining risk: a source with no catalogued 1973 events and an A100-like waveform and moveout cannot be excluded. The A100 stack has only 5 members, and A100 is poorly located in Nakamura 2005. On the bank's own membership tests (R7, R8, R9) and on the evidence above, A100 is the best-supported attribution, and nothing in the folder contradicts it.
