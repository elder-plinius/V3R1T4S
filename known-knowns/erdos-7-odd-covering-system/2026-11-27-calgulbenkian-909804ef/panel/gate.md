# Honesty gate (erdos-7-odd-covering-system)

status: CONFIRMED_PARTIAL
confirmed_scope: A written proof (`object/PROOF.md`), unrefereed and not checked by any proof assistant, together with exact rational recomputation of its finite part (738 scalar certificates, 37 anchored certificates with 29,485 inequalities, and the domination of 6,782 exponent vectors) by two separately written checkers that both pass, shows that every finite covering of Z by residue classes with pairwise distinct odd moduli greater than 1 has L >= 6,557,114,959,770,375, strictly above 11,486,475.

## Gate notes
- Object: object/MANIFEST.json, sha256 909804ef2bf322270ec686552d0952780851e07d3c8471cbfac58507b3c715d6. It lists the nine certificate files in object/certs/ with their sha256, and verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh compares this hash with INDEX.csv before it runs the two checkers.
- Panel verdicts: reproduce `pass: true`, novelty `pass: true`, criterion `pass: true`.
- confirmed_scope is the criterion skeptic's sentence, quoted word for word.
- The three skeptics are Claude Code general-purpose subagents, each started fresh and given only the claim folder. The certificates and the first checker were built by a Gemini model and the second checker by a GPT model, both of a different model family from every skeptic. The proof text was written by a Claude model in another session, which is the skeptics' family; the reproduce and criterion skeptics therefore refereed it step by step.
- The REPRODUCE skeptic wrote its own exact checker from the proof text before reading the package's code and recomputed everything in full, with no sampling: all 738 scalar rows, all 29,485 rows of the 37 anchored certificates, and the enumeration of the 6,782 exponent vectors. Every stored value equals its own as an exact fraction. It refereed the proof line by line and found every lemma, proposition and theorem correct, with one wording gap that does not affect the result: Section 9.4 of the proof does not list key validity among the checks, although Lemma 8.1 needs it; both package checkers and the skeptic's own checker enforce it, and every key satisfies it.
- The CRITERION skeptic recomputed the scalar certificates and the enumeration itself and relied on the exact agreement of the two package checkers for the anchored rows. It tested a null model: with even moduli allowed, where coverings exist, the scalar criterion does not exclude them.
- Status page: the NOVELTY skeptic's single ordinary request for the status page was answered with HTTP 200. Its request for the forum thread was not made (blocked on the reviewing machine before it ran, and not retried), so what the thread shows rests on the browser read recorded in STATUS_CHECK_RECORD.md.
- Largest public bound the novelty skeptic found: L >= 9,704,539,845 (Schroeder, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.22759614, unrefereed). The same contributor's earlier package on this target, with the bound 168,131,152,814,625, was an open, unmerged pull request at the time of this gate; this package does not depend on it.
- Not claimed: that L has at least ten prime factors. The proof is a written argument refereed by the skeptics; its chain of lemmas is not formalised in a proof assistant.
- After the reviews, three text files changed and nothing else: README.md (the sentence under "What" was replaced by the confirmed_scope sentence), STATUS_CHECK_RECORD.md and the-record-we-beat/README.md (the novelty skeptic's own fetches were added). The object, both checkers and the generating code are byte-identical to what the skeptics reviewed.
