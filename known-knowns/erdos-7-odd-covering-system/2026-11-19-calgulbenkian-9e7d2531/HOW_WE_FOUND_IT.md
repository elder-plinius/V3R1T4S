## 1. Live Literature Scout (`KNOWN`)
- **Primary status and prior bounds (`KNOWN`):** Before running any computation, we checked `https://www.erdosproblems.com/7`, its discussion thread `https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/7`, arXiv, Crossref, and Zenodo. Beyond Egeozturk's August 2026 forum bound (`11,486,475`), we found Michael Schroeder's September 2026 unrefereed Zenodo preprint (`10.5281/zenodo.22759614`) claiming `omega(L)` at least `9` and Corollary 1.3 `L` at least `9,704,539,845`, Mian and Siddique's July 2026 Lean 4 note (`arXiv:2607.25628v1`, `L` exceeding `10,000`), and Dai, Li, Li, and Yi's November 2026 preprint (`arXiv:2611.15110v1`, `45` divides `L`), building on Berger-Felzenbaum-Fraenkel (1986/1987), Hough-Nielsen (2019), and Balister-Bollobas-Morris-Sahasrabudhe-Tiba (2021/2022). We therefore froze a strict preregistered novelty bar requiring any `p2` lower bound to beat **both** `11,486,475` and `9,704,539,845` from first principles with zero external axioms.

## 2. Exploratory Diagnostics That Failed First (`NUMERICAL`)
- **Raw Integer Fiber-Pigeonhole Recursion (`NUMERICAL`):** Testing the unweighted prime-by-prime uncovered-residue recursion across all `301,753` odd abundant numbers up to `10^9` left `2,910` unrefuted candidates starting at `675,675 = 3^3 * 5^2 * 7 * 11 * 13`, because unweighted fiber pigeonhole treats pure prime-power moduli on equal footing with mixed moduli instead of completing and deleting pure prime powers first.
- **Unbounded-Exponent 9-Prime `{3, 5}` Anchor Distortion (`NUMERICAL`):** Extending `{3, 5}` modulo-`135` spatial distortion across the first `9` odd primes (`3` through `29`) for arbitrary unbounded prime-power exponents encountered a saddle-point deficit of `-2.16137` `135`-cell units at threshold schedule `(1.8, 3.5, 4.0, 7.0, 8.0, 11.0, 14.0)`, because unbounded geometric positive-depth tails inflate the multiplier product across all `7` stages `7` through `29`.

## 3. What Worked (`PROVED-HERE` and `NUMERICAL`)
- **Finite-Group Prime Replacement and `3^k` Divisibility Band Scaling (`PROVED-HERE`):** Pulling back any hypothetical covering modulo `L` onto the consecutive initial odd primes collapses the entire candidate space at or below `168,131,152,814,624` to `30,147` initial-prime odd abundant profiles, and scaling the exponent of `3` into `(56,043,717,604,874, 168,131,152,814,624]` reduces all `30,147` profiles by divisibility to `7,036` band profiles.
- **Exact Finite-Exponent Scalar Distortion (`PROVED-HERE`):** On those `7,036` band profiles, finite exponents cut off the unbounded positive-depth tails (`e_i = 1` at nearly all primes on high-rank profiles). Pure-deletion (`2,519` band profiles, maximum covered fraction `124651682101/124657827840`) together with exact dyadic-denominator scalar distortion (`4,517` band profiles in `object/lcm_lower_bound_certificate.tsv`, maximum covered measure `16615714572403899891/16710819340101877760`, minimum numerator surplus `95104767697977869`) refutes every candidate below `168,131,152,814,625 = 3^4 * 5^3 * 7^2 * 11^2 * 13 * 17 * 19 * 23 * 29`, exceeding `11486475` by `14637315x` and `9704539845` by `17325x`.
- **Implementation Scale and Runtime (`NUMERICAL`):** Choosing dyadic cap denominators `32` keeps all reduced rational denominators below `2^64` (avoiding hundreds of billions of integers up to `10^14`), so both standalone zero-floating-point verifiers finish end-to-end in approximately `4.6` seconds.
