The portable checker stores evaluated stacks at homogeneous region vertices. For an input leaf of depth d it requires nonnegative evaluated coordinates at most 2^(60-d), with d at most 18 and at most eight vertices. Every continuing all-in transition doubles the poorer stack and subtracts it from the richer stack; the known sign across the region guarantees nonnegative vertex values. Each coordinate therefore remains at most 2^60. Comparison uses integer ordering, not a potentially overflowing difference. Bound numerators are at most 5*6^18 = 507799783342080, within signed 64-bit range; cross multiplication of minima uses signed 128-bit arithmetic. Rational hull geometry is computed with unbounded Python integers. The actual certificates have depth at most 13, at most six vertices per leaf, and maximum homogeneous coordinate 3937.

The entire numeric workload was additionally run with undefined-behavior instrumentation, with all three full transcripts matching. Runtime instrumentation supplements these explicit range arguments; it is not assumed to prove compiler correctness.
