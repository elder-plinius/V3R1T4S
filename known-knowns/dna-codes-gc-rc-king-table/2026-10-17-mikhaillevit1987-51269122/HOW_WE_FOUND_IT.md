# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

The brief was the bank target dna-codes-gc-rc-king-table: beat an unmarked cell of the Gaborit-King
A_4^GC,RC table. One lens built GF(4) linear codes with a built-in reversal symmetry: the 21 points of the
projective plane PG(2,4), paired by a transvection so that the pairing becomes the word reversal, then a
coset shift, a GC = 10 slice, and a maximum clique on the remaining compatibility graph. At (20,15) this gave 16
words in about 11 seconds of search (2026-10-17T14:01Z), against the table's 15.

What failed: affine GF(4) codes at (16,12) only tied 24; punctured projective families at (19,15) and
(18,14) stayed below the table; (20,14) reached 24 against 33.

The literature check then found that the Gaborit-King table had not absorbed the 2012 best-known tables of
Aboluion, Smith and Perkins (33 cells there exceed the online table for n <= 20), so every comparison was made
against those tables too; for (20,15) all sources give 15.
