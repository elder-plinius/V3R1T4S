## 1. Stage 0 X-Ray of the `herH3` `one_vs_rest` Extrapolation Cliff

In `LSSI-ETH/plmfit` (`data/herH3/herH3_data_full.csv`, `36,560` rows of 449-aa Trastuzumab heavy chains), the `one_vs_rest` split trains on only `174` single-mutant sequences (`173` `train` + `1` `validation`, Hamming distance $d=1$ from wild-type `WT10 = "WGGDGFYAMD"` at positions `99..108`) and evaluates on `36,386` multi-mutant sequences ($d \in \{2..10\}$, with $85\%$ at $d \in \{6,7,8,9\}$).

Dissecting the `174` training rows revealed two structural bottlenecks that cause large fine-tuned language models (`ProGen2-XL + LoRA`, `6.4B` parameters, `MCC = 0.3898`) and simple 1-body PSSMs (`Bet #1`, `L-PTCB-001`, `MCC = 0.3006`, `AUROC = 0.7368`) to stall:
1. **Constant IgG1 Fc Tail Dilution vs. Contextual VH-CDRH3 Epistasis:** `439` of the `449` heavy-chain residues are identical across all `36,560` variants. Evaluating frozen `facebook/esm2_t6_8M_UR50D` (`7.51M` parameters `<= 10M`, `p1`) on the wild-typeperturbation table (`Bet #3`, `L-PTCB-003`) lifted `Test AUROC` from `0.7205` (`Bet #2`) to `0.7728` (`Oracle MCC = 0.3915`), and severing the constant Fc tail to evaluate `esm2_t6_8M_UR50D` on the 44-aa `VH-CDRH3` window `[78:122]` (`Bet #5`, `L-PTCB-005`) jumped `Test AUROC` to **`0.7901`** (`Test AP = 0.6209`, `Oracle MCC = 0.4275`).
2. **Additive Log-Odds Drift Under $d=1 \to d=8$ Extrapolation:** When extrapolating from single mutants ($9/10$ wild-type residues) to octuple mutants ($2/10$ wild-type residues), any uncalibrated $d=1$ threshold (`tau = 0` in `Bet #5` or unnormalized mutated-site sum in `Bet #7`) miscalibrates the $d \in \{2..10\}$ decision boundary.

## 2. Three Structural Leaps (`L-PTCB-001` $\to$ `L-PTCB-008` $\to$ `L-PTCB-011`)

1. **Contextual VH-CDRH3 Window `[78:122]` + 5-Estimator Empirical-Bayes Site Posterior (`L-PTCB-005`..`008`):** Extracting frozen `facebook/esm2_t6_8M_UR50D` (`7.51M` parameters `<= 10M`, `p1`) representations across the 44-aa VH framework + CDR-H3 window `[78:122]` lets self-attention heads detect multi-mutant steric/electrostatic clashes directly. Combining 5 site estimators (`p_bal`, `p_unb`, BLOSUM62 kernel `p_ker`, cross-site `p_ctx1`, and L2-logistic `p_sh`) produces a well-calibrated $10 \times 20$ site probability table $P_{\text{eb}}[k, a]$.
2. **Law of the Unconscious Statistician (`LOTUS`) `10-Fold Stratified CV` Threshold Calibration (`Bet #8` / `Track A`, `L-PTCB-008`):** In `L-PTCB-008`, we replaced the analytical 0-vs-1 defect midpoint (`Bet #6`, `L-PTCB-006`, `MCC = 0.4255`) with a strictly cross-validated threshold rule (`argmax_th OOF_MCC` inside `10-Fold Stratified CV` on the `174` training rows) by evaluating each held-out fold's single-mutant observations against viable multi-mutant background contexts drawn strictly from that training fold's $P_{\text{eb}}^{(-f)}$ distribution (`S_0` vs `S_1`). With equal `1/5 = 0.20` portfolio weights and the parameter-free loop free energy $S_{\text{canonical}}(x) = \frac{1}{2}(\mu_\ell(x) + m_{-1}(x)) - C_{\text{pair}}(x)$, `Track A` achieved `Test MCC = 0.408391 +- 0.000411`, `Test AP = 0.622932 +- 0.000615`, and an inductive-to-oracle threshold gap of just `0.001402`.
3. **Empirical-Variance Regularized Out-Of-Fold Brier Stacking & Inverse-Root-SD Loop Potential (`Bet #11` / `Track B`, `L-PTCB-011`):** Diagnostic comparison of the 5 base estimators on the `174` training rows after `Bet #9` (`L-PTCB-009`, `MCC = 0.3610`) and `Bet #10` (`L-PTCB-010`, `MCC = 0.4056`) showed that the 3 site-local estimators (`p_bal`, `p_unb`, `p_ker`) have much higher out-of-fold accuracy than the 2 global cross-site regularizers (`p_ctx1`, `p_sh`). Minimizing Out-Of-Fold Brier Loss regularized toward the `1/5` naive portfolio by the parameter-free empirical label variance $\text{Var}(y_{\text{tr}})$ inside `10-Fold Stratified CV` on the `174` training rows converged across all 3 seeds to $w^* = [0.20, 0.30, 0.20, 0.15, 0.15]$, lifting 3-seed `Test MCC` to **`0.421004 +- 0.001735`** (`+0.031204` above `0.3898`), `Test AUROC` to **`0.789510 +- 0.000669`**, and `Test AP` to **`0.624177 +- 0.000884`**.
