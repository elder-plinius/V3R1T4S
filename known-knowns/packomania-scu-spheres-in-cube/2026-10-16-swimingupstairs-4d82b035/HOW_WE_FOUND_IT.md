# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
A V3R1T4S run audited Packomania's scu coordinate files and found many rows just above plateaus where a posted packing has free holes. Starting from Lu Peng's posted scu880.txt, spheres were inserted into the largest holes and the packing was re-inflated with a penalty / sparse-LP method. Most chain steps beyond 886 fell below the table or behind Moki & Julio's queued values; 884, 883, 882 and 886 beat both and passed a three-skeptic panel (after a paperwork repair round). The N=881 object (first submitted as #263) came from the same chain stopped after one insertion and passed its own three-skeptic panel after two paperwork repair rounds. #236, #237 and #263 were folded into this package at the maintainer's request.
