# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

Brief: certify an upper bound on the optimal selfish-mining revenue in SSZ's model below the bank bar (0.70099 at alpha = 0.45) with an exact dual certificate for the over-paying MDP N_rho^T, as the bank's success criterion describes.

First attempt (T = 250, rho = 0.67045) built N_rho^T exactly as printed and passed. A fresh REPRODUCE skeptic then showed eq. (28) uses an impossible E[a0 | return]. Repairing only that left the float root at about 0.670445, but a deterministic series for the last-exit time showed Lemma 13 / eq. (22) was also off by a factor in its K term (E[psi] = 99 at (0,0), alpha = 0.45, versus 49.5 implied by the paper). With both fixed the T = 250 root moved to 0.6707737, so the old point died, and larger T was needed. A new label-collapsed Gauss-Seidel builder reached roots of 0.6700578610 at T = 2000; its bias vector, rounded to integers (scale 2^40) at a gain just above the optimum, gives the exact certificate. An exact policy certificate at T = 1500 gives REV > 0.67005786 (not shipped). Dead ends: certificates below the root fail (negative controls), and alpha = 0.475 at rho = 0.817 does not survive the correction at T = 250 (a later T = 2000 attempt reached 0.8083, outside this package). A three-skeptic panel (fresh contexts) then reproduced, novelty-checked and criterion-checked the claim; their files are in panel/.
