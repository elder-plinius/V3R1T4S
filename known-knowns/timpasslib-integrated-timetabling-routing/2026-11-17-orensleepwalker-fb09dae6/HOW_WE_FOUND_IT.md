# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
We wrote an evaluator for TimPassLib from the benchmark's description and checked that it reproduces all 27 posted record totals. A search that re-solves slices of the timetable exactly with CP-SAT while every passenger group keeps its current route cannot move the posted Hamburg record: freeing all events, CP-SAT proves it optimal for its routes (a fixed point). Letting each affected passenger group choose among a few candidate routes inside the slice, and starting from the 2023 timetable instead of the record, climbed past the record in minutes (140,833,937). An eight-job batch (several seeds, three different starts) ended five times at 140,833,625, always the same timetable up to a global time shift; one job ended exactly on the posted best timetable, and from the posted best itself nothing was found. A teammate ran the batch; the saved logs and best files of every job are in how-we-built-it/logs/.
