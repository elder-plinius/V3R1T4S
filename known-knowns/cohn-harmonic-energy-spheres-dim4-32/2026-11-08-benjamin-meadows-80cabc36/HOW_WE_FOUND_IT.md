# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

An unattended search lane (a series of AI coding sessions in Docker containers) worked through the live harmonic energy table at https://cohn.mit.edu/harmonic-energy/ for n from 4 to 32, with the table values as targets. The lane swept each dimension column from N = 64 down to N = 44 with `worker3.py`. For each N it tried deleting each point of the best (N+1) configuration, then adding one point at a deep hole of the best (N-1) configuration, then basin hops for 25 seconds. For (61, 21) the hit came from the addition step: the posted 2021 configuration for (60, 21) plus one point at its deepest hole relaxed (L-BFGS-B on the sphere, exponent 19) to 4.1039483024, below the table 4.1191657308 (how-we-built-it/src/hit.log, lane log col21.log lines 22-23 and 43). The lane's own summary credits this win to point deletion. Its log says addition, and this account follows the log.

What failed: deleting one point from the (62, 21) configuration gave nothing. A second pass that diversified the (N+1) minima before deleting gave no new gains in this column. Deleting two points gave nothing on (60, 21) and (62, 21). Basin hopping alone from posted configurations found nothing.
