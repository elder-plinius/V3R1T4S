# How it was found

The contributor's team generalised the diagonal-layer construction from three to four dimensions, with a symmetry reduction by a group of order 36 at cross-section (3,3,2), and ran four Collatz-Wielandt iterations from the all-ones vector in the largest sector (sector 9, 1404 orbits). The result is a vector and a ratio whose logarithm divided by 36 is 0.584014769338. The author printed this as 0.5840147693, which is rounded down; during checking the correct rounded-up figure 0.5840147694 was recorded and the checker changed to round up. The object file itself was left byte for byte as the author wrote it, so its header still shows the old figure.
