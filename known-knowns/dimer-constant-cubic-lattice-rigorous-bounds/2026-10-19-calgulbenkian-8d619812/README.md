# Cubic-lattice dimer constant: upper bound 0.452010 at cross-section (5,5), 1.2e-4 below He's 0.452130 (Full Win)

## What

Subject to the reproduce skeptic's exact recomputation of the 13 sector ratios, the folder's explicit test vectors, transfer-layer definition and written derivation (He's method and Corollary 3.4 at a converged iterate at cross-section (5,5)) give l3 <= 0.4520095976 (0.452010 rounded up), 1.2e-4 below He's published 0.452130 as of 2026-10-19; the method is He's own, not new.

## Check it yourself

From this package folder, run:

```sh
sh verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh
```

- **Requirements**: python3 3.8 or later, standard library only for the default sector-12 check; a C compiler (cc, libc and pthreads only) for the check of all 13 sectors. Without a C compiler only sector 12 is checked and the script says so.
- **Time**: about 8 minutes (8 min 23 s measured by the contributor on a loaded 28-core Mac; about 2 minutes of the checker is the Python check of sector 12, the rest is the C check of all 13 sectors).
- **Expected last line**: `ALL CHECKS PASS`.

## Not claimed

- The method is Qidong He's own (Theorem 2.1 with Proposition 3.3 and Corollary 3.4 of arXiv 2607.28810), not new. What is new is only a deeper Collatz-Wielandt iteration (a converged iterate) at the same cross-section (5,5) that He computed at p = 2.
- The result is conditional on the reproduce skeptic's exact recomputation of the 13 sector ratios, the folder's explicit test vectors, the transfer-layer definition and the written derivation.
- Only the certificate vector for the largest sector (12) is the author's. The vectors for sectors 0 to 11 were generated by the builder of the package, not by the author, and the reproduce skeptic checked all of them (the largest of them is 0.4492950875, in sector 11). They are shipped in verify-it-yourself/sectors/.
- The cross-family reviewer (a model of a different family) saw the written derivations only, not the certificates. The three panel skeptics and the object's builder (Gemini, Argon) are different model families, but none of this is peer review.
- It is an upper bound only. No claim on the lower bound of l3, on other cross-sections, or on the overall best possible bound from this method.
- No newer bound than He's was found by the arXiv searches on 2026-10-19, but the citation indexes did not yet cover He's paper, and a days-old preprint or a newer version of He's paper could change the comparator.
- The package ships a lossless split of the 1.9 MB certificate into three files of at most 900,000 bytes each, because every file must be at most 1 MB. The concatenation (in the order part1, part2, part3) has sha256 8d6198121bb490e8f44f352114144556fd75cc33aa98b4f698335ca274342661, which is the file the panel checked.
