# How we found it

Contributor text, unverified.
A one-glider sweep hit published 6-glider precursor xs25_0ml1u8z643032ak8 at rotation 1,
lane 11, phase 0. The new glider builds this distinct 28-cell object. Shinjuku route composition
with pre=32 made the incoming-gliders-only RLE, reproduced byte-for-byte in this confirmation.
The search happened in the Nov 3 harvest, before this confirmation preregistration.
This is credited reuse of a known precursor, not an independent discovery of its route.
An exploratory no-proper-stable-subset test was too strong: five embedded stable subsets exist,
but none has a stable complement. The correct exact partition test passes, and both target
islands are unstable separately. Embedded still lifes do not by themselves make the whole pseudo.
First-synthesis scope is relative to the live Catagolue table criterion, not global historical priority.

Definition reference opened during confirmation: Matthew Cook, Still Life Theory, https://www.paradise.caltech.edu/~cook/Workshop/CAs/2DOutTot/Life/StillLife/StillLifeTheory.html — strict means no partition of the islands into two independently stable subsets. Here there are exactly two islands, and neither is stable separately, so no higher-cardinality island partition is possible either. The all-cell stable-subset/complement search is an additional exhaustive check.
