# How We Found It (`2026-10-21-mizopl-5aa962ff`)

*Note: contributor text, unverified.*

1. **Held-Out Split, Collator Protocol (`80/10/10` vs `[MASK]`-Only), and Composition Overlap Analysis**:
   - We applied the `SEED = 2` 80/20 document split from `SLAB-NLP/Akk/preprocessing/main_preprocess.py` (`18,568` training rows, `5,412,259` valid tokens) and stripped all test documents sharing `id_text`, 6-digit P/Q/X IDs, `bert_input` text, or full token sequences with the training split (`1,875` held-out test documents; see `object/test_tensors_recipe.md`), then froze `N = 27,650` masked positions at `torch.manual_seed(42)`.
   - In `SLAB-NLP/Akk/akkadian_bert/evaluate_bert.py` (`eval_compute_metrics` and `compute_mrr_and_all_hit_ks`), evaluation scores all `pred.label_ids != -100` positions produced by `DataCollatorForLanguageModelingAkkadian` (`22,205` `[MASK]`-replaced, `2,723` kept-token, and `2,722` random-token positions). On all `27,650` positions, `MBERT+Akk` achieves `Hit@5 = 0.87700` (`0.87032` on the `25,957` non-missing positions directly on the shipped CSVs, and `0.87777` across all `27,220` masked whole-word units); on the `22,205` `[MASK]`-only positions (`input_ids == 103`), `MBERT+Akk` is `0.86463` (`0.86560` on `[MASK]`-only whole-word units), and on the `21,459` single-token whole words (`is_single_word == 1`) it is `0.86770` on the raw shipped CSVs (`0.86905` when missing-sign candidate IDs `{4, 5, 17}` are suppressed).
   - We also audited composition (`Q` number) and witness overlaps between test and training documents: removing the `46` `Q`-number test documents (`N = 27,122`), the `228` `Q`-number or shared-title test documents (`N = 24,454`), or the `242` test documents sharing a $\ge 6$-word span with any training `Q`-number composition (`N = 23,781`), `CLSS-Akk-v3` maintains `Hit@5 = 0.92235`, `0.92034`, and `0.92162` vs `MBERT+Akk` `0.87844`, `0.87744`, and `0.88239`.
2. **Boundary-Deduplicated 53-Template C Engine (`how-we-built-it/clss_akk_engine_v3.c`)**:
   - We indexed the `18,568` training documents into a `2^23`-slot open-addressed hash table over `53` contiguous and gapped bilateral window templates (`radius <= 5`), skipped out-of-bounds boundary templates to eliminate `[CLS]`/`[SEP]` multi-counting, mapped `10%` random-corrupted non-Akkadian BERT subwords (`UNK_C`) to `MASK_C`, added rare-anchor TF-IDF parallel-passage retrieval and two-pass belief propagation, and fused log-probabilities with `MBERT+Akk` ($\alpha = 11.5$), reaching `Hit@5 = 0.92156` (`0.91263` on `[MASK]`-only) and `MRR = 0.85593` (`0.84015` on `[MASK]`-only).
