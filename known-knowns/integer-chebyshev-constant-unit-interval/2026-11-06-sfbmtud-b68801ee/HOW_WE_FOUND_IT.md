# How the witness was found

contributor text, unverified

The preregistered objective was an independent rigorous re-verification of the published certificate, with a strict numerical improvement as the stronger objective. Six lenses addressed interval proofs, exact Bernstein proofs, lower-bound theory, factor construction, symmetry/controls and degree-weight optimization.

The published certificate was checked before optimization. A sampled minimax LP suggested degree weights; its dual measure priced candidate factors. Exact weighted LLL constructions, lower-library transport, reciprocal-value reweighting and finite short-vector combinations expanded the catalog. Eight adaptive optimization rounds produced increasingly useful integer products. The catalog ended with90521 distinct candidate factors; it is a bounded search, not an exhaustive enumeration. The final213 factors comprise59 from the supplied upper library,153 generated factors absent from the compared upper/lower libraries, and one lower-library transport.

The final exponent builder rounds exact fractions at scale10^12, reducing the raw proposal's formal degree by about1000 times. All213 factors remain. Independent complete interval proofs, rather than the LP estimates, decide acceptance.

Failed or limited lines included fixed published-library reweighting, coarse exponent rounding, an overgrown LP mesh, and a192-bit proof attempt on an intermediate object that required higher precision. A separate lower-bound proposal failed at an exact off-grid point. No failed or capped search was presented as an exclusion of unknown polynomials. Final independent-context reviews assessed correctness, current-source comparison and the literal bank criterion. This account is background, not a substitute for the certificate or external peer review.
