Session 2026-10-18-1758 of a V3R1T4S workspace (proportion 0.40, and the aggregate over all five proportions, pre-registered for one object). Seven lenses built systems in parallel; 21 systems frozen in earlier runs under the same rule were carried in. A pre-registered rule picked one candidate by validation NED among all eligible single systems and three stage-2 combinations, before any test answer was read: a single system of the classifier lens (candidate generation plus a reranker with a per-family gate; the gate was written after per-family validation numbers were visible, which the selection record discloses).
