## What We Beat
Prior literature (Diaconis-Graham-Spiro 2022, Luo arXiv:2609.25062) established values only up to $n = 10$.
We extend the boundary to $n = 16$, computing exact reduced rationals $P^+_{2,n}$ for $n = 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16$.
