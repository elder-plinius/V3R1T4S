4. Point the panel should weigh: sequence.txt is not independent of the baseline source. It is the published L=339 sequence with its last element removed, an operation (eta_4) that is defined in the same paper but not tabulated for L=338. Under the bank's rule (compare integer energy with the best published energy for that length) this is not prior art, so I rate it NEW; whether a one-step edit of a published sequence is an acceptable record is a panel question, not a novelty finding.

Sci. 2024, Digital Signal Processing 2025) were not read, though their arXiv versions were read by the claim author and by me for the lengths they cover, and the group's own page lists nothing for even lengths 305 to 399; (b) the 2013 even and odd records pages named on Knauer's page could not be found anywhere; (c) arXiv papers that give a length-338 value only in full text and not in the abstract cannot be found with the API; (d) the full texts of the small-N arXiv papers were not read; (e) a competing L=338 pull request may appear later.
