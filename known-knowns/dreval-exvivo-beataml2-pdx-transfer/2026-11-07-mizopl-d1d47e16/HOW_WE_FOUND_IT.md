## 1. Diagnosing Why Out-of-the-Box `CTRPv2` $\to$ Ex Vivo Transfer Fails (`R2_norm < 0`)

When a drug-response predictor trained on `CTRPv2` (`776` immortalized cancer cell lines $\times$ `545` compounds, `372,469` `LN_IC50_curvecurator` measurements) is evaluated zero-shot on `BeatAML2` (`19,823` rows/split across `484` primary AML patient blasts and `154` drugs) or `PDX_Bruna` (`1,046` rows/split across `25` breast-cancer PDX explants and `100` drugs), DrEval's mean-effect-normalized $R^2_{\text{norm}}$ is negative for every baseline model (`-0.09514` on `BeatAML2` and `-0.39248` on `PDX_Bruna` for `SchurBilinearExVivoDRP`).

Inspecting the unlabeled assay protocols, gene-expression platforms, and compound libraries across `CTRPv2`, `BeatAML2`, and `PDX_Bruna` revealed three distinct, quantifiable structural shifts — each estimable **without touching held-out ex vivo `y_true` labels**:

1. **Factor (i) — Assay & Readout Concentration-Window Shift ($\Delta^{(i)}_{\text{assay}}(c,d)$):**
   `CurveCurator` fits $\ln(\text{IC}_{50})$ relative to each assay plate's tested dilution window `[min_dose_M, max_dose_M]`. `CTRPv2` screens compounds up to a median maximum concentration of $66\ \mu\text{M}$ ($\overline{\ln(\text{max\_dose}_{\mu\text{M}})} = +4.0177$), whereas `BeatAML2` screens up to $10\ \mu\text{M}$ ($\overline{\ln(\text{max\_dose}_{\mu\text{M}})} = +2.2202$) and `PDX_Bruna` screens up to $4\text{--}5\ \mu\text{M}$ ($\overline{\ln(\text{max\_dose}_{\mu\text{M}})} = +1.3145$). Because `314` drugs in `CTRPv2` were screened across multiple concentration windows across batches, we fitted a **within-drug Fixed-Effect (`FE`) linear regression** of `LN_IC50_curvecurator` on $(\ln\text{min\_dose}_{\mu\text{M}}, \ln\text{max\_dose}_{\mu\text{M}})$ strictly on each `CTRPv2` training split ($\hat{\beta}_{\text{min}}^{\text{FE}} = +0.000642$, $\hat{\beta}_{\text{max}}^{\text{FE}} = +0.376184$). Applying the resulting unlabeled per-row shift $\Delta^{(i)}_{\text{assay}}(c,d)$ **on its own** to `y_pred_schur` closes **`-1.5013` MSE (`+0.10800` `R2_norm`, reaching `+0.01286 +- 0.00444` across `10/10` splits)** on `BeatAML2` and **`-4.1895` MSE (`+0.36758` `R2_norm`, reaching `-0.02490 +- 0.01705`, or `+0.04462 +- 0.00346` under pooled `CTRPv2` OLS)** on `PDX_Bruna`, making Factor `(i)` the **#1 dominant single factor** on both cohorts.
2. **Factor (ii) — Expression-Platform Shift ($\Delta^{(ii)}_{\text{platform}}$):**
   While `BeatAML2` uses RNA-seq (like `CTRPv2`), `PDX_Bruna` profiles gene expression using **Illumina HumanHT-12 v4 BeadChip microarrays**. In `SchurBilinearExVivoDRP`, cross-sample standardization (`std_view`) using `CTRPv2` RNA-seq mean/SD induces an unlabeled cohort shift between the multi-view prediction (`y_pred_schur`) and the single-view KRR prediction (`y_pred_raw_krr`): $\Delta^{(ii)}_{\text{platform}} = -\mathbb{E}[\hat{y}_{\text{schur}} - \hat{y}_{\text{raw\_krr}}]$ (`-0.14191 +- 0.05285` on `BeatAML2`; `-0.36781 +- 0.08580` on `PDX_Bruna`). Applying $\Delta^{(ii)}_{\text{platform}}$ **on its own** closes **`-1.9630` MSE (`+0.17224` `R2_norm`)** on `PDX_Bruna` (**Rank #2** on `PDX_Bruna`) and **`-0.1982` MSE (`+0.01426` `R2_norm`)** on `BeatAML2` (**Rank #3** on `BeatAML2`, where within-sample van der Waerden rank-normal alignment already lifts `Pearson_norm` by `+0.09467` over `RawPlatformKRR_Ablation`).
3. **Factor (iii) — Drug Overlap Between `CTRPv2` and the Ex Vivo Screen ($\Delta^{(iii)}_{\text{overlap}}(c,d)$):**
   Approximately `400` of `CTRPv2`'s `545` compounds are inactive chemical diversity probes ($\mu_{\text{CTRP}} = +1.8530\ \ln(\mu\text{M})$), whereas `BeatAML2` (`79` shared, `75` unseen) and `PDX_Bruna` (`49` shared, `51` unseen) screen clinical oncology inhibitors. On unseen drugs ($d \notin D_{\text{CTRPv2}^{(k)}}$), predictions revert toward the probe-dominated `CTRPv2` global mean, jumping by `+1.29523` $\ln(\mu\text{M})$ on `BeatAML2` and `+0.75936` $\ln(\mu\text{M})$ on `PDX_Bruna` relative to shared drugs. Correcting this unlabeled unseen-drug prior mismatch **on its own** closes **`-1.3121` MSE (`+0.09439` `R2_norm`, lifting `Pearson_norm` from `+0.08461` to `+0.16529`)** on `BeatAML2` (**Rank #2 Co-Dominant** on `BeatAML2`) and **`-1.4511` MSE (`+0.12732` `R2_norm`)** on `PDX_Bruna` (**Rank #3** on `PDX_Bruna`).
4. **Cumulative Effect (`(i) + (iii) + (ii)`):**
   When all three unlabeled corrections are applied cumulatively to `y_pred_schur`, 10-split mean `R2_norm` reaches **`+0.05710 +- 0.00305` (`10/10` splits `> 0`, `Pearson_norm = +0.24186 +- 0.00739`, `MSE = 13.1068 +- 0.0422`)** on all `19,823` `BeatAML2` rows and **`+0.10159 +- 0.00598` (`10/10` splits `> 0`, `Pearson_norm = +0.35691 +- 0.01104`, `MSE = 10.2395 +- 0.0612`)** on all `1,046` `PDX_Bruna` rows.
