# Computational proof and exact scope

PROVED-HERE by complete finite enumeration, subject to the supplied programs and arithmetic implementation being correct: There is no injective labeling of the 19 vertices of two copies of K10 sharing exactly one vertex by residues modulo 90 for which the 90 within-clique edge sums are all distinct modulo 90.

Let q=90. The graph consists of two K10 cliques sharing exactly a center c, and has 19 vertices and 90 edges. A harmonious labeling is an injective map to Z_90 whose edge sums are all different.

1. Replace all vertex labels f(v) by f(v)-f(c). Distinctness is preserved; each edge sum is translated by -2f(c), a bijection of Z_90. Hence c=0 loses no labeling. Division by two is never used.
2. Sort the nine noncentral labels of each clique, since these vertices have identical graph roles. All normalized label sets are represented. The least of nine labels in 1..89 ranges through 1..81. No multiplication-by-unit or interchange quotient is used.
3. A valid single clique has exactly 45 distinct edge sums. Appending label x creates precisely the translated old vertex labels V+x modulo 90. These are mutually distinct. Rejecting an intersection with previous edge sums is exact and monotone under extension. Rejecting a prefix with too few remaining candidates is sound.
4. Enumerate all normalized single cliques and associate their sum masks. Two cliques give the full labeling exactly when their masks complement each other in the 90-bit residue universe. Complementarity also forces disjoint noncentral vertex sets: a shared vertex label x would give the shared spoke sum 0+x. One representative per sum mask is sufficient in the first research method. The independent checker instead retains all vertex sets and checks their intersection explicitly.
5. The independently reproduced complete enumeration yields 1702800 cliques and 1571472 distinct masks, with zero complementary pairs. Therefore no normalized full labeling exists. Steps 1 and 2 then exclude every labeling, not merely a normalized subset or a sample.
6. The second research program independently constructs both cliques with candidate propagation. Its candidate invariant forbids exactly collisions with sums already used, and increasing order avoids permutations.
