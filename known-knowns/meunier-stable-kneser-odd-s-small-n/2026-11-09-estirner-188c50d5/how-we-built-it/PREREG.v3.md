## Why a v3

The NOVELTY skeptic (panel/novelty.md, pass: false) found that Jonsson's 2012 manuscript "On the chromatic number of
generalized stable Kneser graphs" (still public through the Wayback Machine), Theorem 1.3 with t = 2 and
d = floor(2n/5), together with Schrijver's theorem, gives chi(KG(n,3)_{5-stab}) >= ceil(n(d-4)/d). That equals n - 10
for n = 18, 19, 20, 21 and for every n >= 23, but only 6 at n = 17 and 11 at n = 22. I checked the arithmetic. So,
on the primary source, the open n for (k,s) = (3,5) are n = 17 and n = 22, not 17..21: the status reading of v1/v2
came from a paraphrase in arXiv 2607.12912 ("n >= 2s(k-1)+2").
