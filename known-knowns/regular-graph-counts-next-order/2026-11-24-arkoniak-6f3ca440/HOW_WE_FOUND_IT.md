# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

The brief: extend the OEIS tables of connected regular graphs by one exact term, with two independent methods. Method A, a C port of the Cheng-Xu histogram recurrence with CRT over word-size primes, gave U_5(N) to N=42 quickly. Method D, a separate per-cycle-type count (a vertex DP for fixed points and 2-cycles, a symmetric count-vector DP for longer cycles, modulo 2^521-1), was the slow part; a C port of the short-part DP made degree 5 at 30 vertices fit in one night on 2 cores. Both methods agree on U_5(30), and the same method-D code reproduces every published term of A165626 and A006821. The claim waited two days for a novelty check of sources the first panel could not read. The same pipeline gave A002851(21) (cubic) and A006822(25) (sextic) earlier.
