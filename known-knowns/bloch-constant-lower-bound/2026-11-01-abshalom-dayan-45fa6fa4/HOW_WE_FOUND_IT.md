# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
Wikstrom's proof splits on |a3|. His "away" branch cost 11.4 million boxes at his gain target, and his near branch bound the result at 0.015304. We replaced the measure-side cover by a primal one: normalise a2 and a3 by symmetry, cover the three real parameters in dyadic boxes, and give each box an explicit disc centre, or exclude it with inequality (20). Hundreds to 14,000 boxes suffice. Better point cuts then extended the positivity radius, and splitting |a3| into pieces with their own radii raised the near case to 0.0155. Search code is float; only the replayed inequalities count.
