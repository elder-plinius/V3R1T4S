## 1. The Brief and Observation
Perminov's `addition_reduced_ZT` catalogue stores straight-line programs (SLPs) for ternary fast matrix multiplication schemes generated by iterative pair-selection heuristics (`g`, `ga`, `wr`, `gr`, `gp`, `gi`, `mix`). Two sources of sub-optimality remain for formats with $\max(n_1, n_2, n_3) \le 8$:
1. Single-step greedy pair selection breaks ties myopically and does not re-cover residual linear forms using previously synthesized fresh variables.
2. Walking the rank-preserving ternary flip graph (`flip` moves that preserve rank $m$ and ternary coefficients in $\{-1, 0, 1\}$) changes the sparsity and shared-pair density of $(U, V, W)$ before CSE is applied.

## 2. Lens 1 (`c-evaluator-optimizer`) and Lens 2 (`representation-absorber`)
1. **Lens 1 (`how-we-built-it/cse_opt.c`, `how-we-built-it/run_lens1.py`)**: We built a multi-threaded native C (`clang -O3 -pthread`) CSE optimizer combining 2-step lookahead tie-breaking, randomized multi-start search over tie-break weights, and exact sub-row re-covering over synthesized fresh variables.
2. **Lens 2 (`how-we-built-it/flip_cse.py`)**: We combined rank-preserving ternary flip-graph walks with our C CSE optimizer, accepting ternary flips that lower either naive additions `cn` or reduced additions `cr`.

On `2x4x7` (`m = 45`), this pipeline reduced the straight-line program addition count from `174` (`2x4x7_m45_cr174_cn308_ZT_reduced.json`) to **`164`** (`fv = 50`, `cn = 282`), verified symbolically over $\mathbb{Z}$ across all `3,136` bilinear equations.
