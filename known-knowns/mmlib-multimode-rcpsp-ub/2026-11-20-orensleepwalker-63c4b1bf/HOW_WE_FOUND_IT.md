# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

A scout showed that plain CP-SAT could not even match the best-known values (the non-renewable budgets stall it), and that many open MMLIB+ instances still carry 2018 values with no posted schedule. Phase A built a budget-feasible mode-mix model plus a fast schedule generator with forward-backward improvement (about 6% above the best-known values). The search phases added a compiled memetic algorithm with mode trading and a penalty-based simulated annealing, and handed the best few mode vectors to an exact fixed-mode CP-SAT solve. Structural signals (low order strength, 100 activities, 6 or 9 modes) pointed to where the old values were weakest; most wins came from there. Re-timing or locally repairing posted schedules gave nothing; the 2023 and 2026 values held up far better than the 2018 ones.
