# brouwer-qary-unrestricted-a3-a4-a5: A4(12,7) >= 320

## What

`object/A4-12-7-320words.txt` (sha256 7a882f80...) lists 320 distinct words of length n = 12 over q = 4 symbols with minimum Hamming distance 7 over all pairs, which is strictly above the lower bound 256 (ub 1167, not `=`) for A4(12,7) as extracted in RECORD.md from the quaternary table fetched 2026-11-20T14:01:15Z; page fidelity and the literature search are left to the other reviewers.

In numbers: 320 words of length 12 over {0, ..., 3}, minimum Hamming distance 7, against the table's lower bound 256 (upper bound 1167), a margin of 64 words. This package contains one object only: object/A4-12-7-320words.txt (one word per line, after a one-line description starting with `#`).

The construction, in words: the union of the orbits of the representatives listed in verify-it-yourself/rebuild.py under the group of order 48 generated by g1: the symbol in position i moves to position i+2 mod 12 and is replaced by s1(x), where s1 fixes 0 and sends 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 1; and g2: the symbol in position i moves to position 11i mod 12 and is replaced by s2(x), where s2 exchanges 0 and 1 and fixes 2 and 3.

## Check it yourself
