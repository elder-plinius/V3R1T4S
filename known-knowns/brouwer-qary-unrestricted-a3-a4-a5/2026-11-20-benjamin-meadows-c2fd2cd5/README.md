## What

`object/A4-12-8-145words.txt` (sha256 c2fd2cd5…) is a code of 145 distinct words of length n = 12 over q = 4 symbols with minimum Hamming distance 8 (all 10440 pairs checked), exceeding the A4(12,8) lower bound 128 (ub 240) shown in RECORD.md's extraction of https://aeb.win.tue.nl/codes/quaternary-1.html fetched 2026-11-20T14:01:15Z; live-page match and literature novelty are left to the other reviewers.

In numbers: 145 words of length 12 over {0, ..., 3}, minimum Hamming distance 8, against the table's lower bound 128 (upper bound 240), a margin of 17 words. This package contains one object only: object/A4-12-8-145words.txt (one word per line, after a one-line description starting with `#`).

The construction, in words: the union of the orbits of the representatives listed in verify-it-yourself/rebuild.py under the group of order 36 generated by A: the symbol in position i moves to position 5i+1 mod 12, symbols unchanged; and B: the symbol in position i moves to position i+2 mod 12 and is replaced by s(x), where s fixes 0 and sends 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 1.
