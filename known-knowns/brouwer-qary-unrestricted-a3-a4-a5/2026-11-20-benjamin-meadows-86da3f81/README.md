# brouwer-qary-unrestricted-a3-a4-a5: A3(15,10) >= 31

## What

`object/A3-15-10-31words.txt` (sha256 86da3f81...) is a set of 31 distinct words of length n = 15 over q = 3 symbols with minimum Hamming distance 10 (all 465 pairs checked), so A3(15,10) >= 31, which exceeds the lower bound 27 (upper bound 39) that RECORD.md extracts from the ternary table fetched 2026-11-20T14:01:14Z; the live-table and literature clauses rest on the other reviewers.

In numbers: 31 words of length 15 over {0, ..., 2}, minimum Hamming distance 10, against the table's lower bound 27 (upper bound 39), a margin of 4 words. This package contains one object only: object/A3-15-10-31words.txt (one word per line, after a one-line description starting with `#`).

The construction, in words: the union of the orbits of the representatives listed in verify-it-yourself/rebuild.py under the group of order 30 generated by the cyclic shift: the symbol in position i moves to position i+1 mod 15, symbols unchanged; and the symbol exchange 1 <-> 2 at every coordinate at once (positions unchanged).
