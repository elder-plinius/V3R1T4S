# brouwer-qary-unrestricted-a3-a4-a5: A4(11,7) >= 145

## What

`object/A4-11-7-145words.txt` (sha256 96ddddae...) is a list of 145 distinct words of length n = 11 over q = 4 symbols with all-pairs minimum Hamming distance 7, exceeding the lower bound 128 for A4(11,7) shown in RECORD.md's extraction of the table fetched 2026-11-20T14:01:15Z, as the puncture of the companion A4(12,8) code; live-table currency and the literature search are left to the novelty reviewer.

In numbers: 145 words of length 11 over {0, ..., 3}, minimum Hamming distance 7, against the table's lower bound 128 (upper bound 320), a margin of 17 words. This package contains one object only: object/A4-11-7-145words.txt (one word per line, after a one-line description starting with `#`).

The construction, in words: the union of the orbits of the representatives listed in verify-it-yourself/rebuild.py under the group of order 36 generated by A: the symbol in position i moves to position 5i+1 mod 12, symbols unchanged; and B: the symbol in position i moves to position i+2 mod 12 and is replaced by s(x), where s fixes 0 and sends 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 1, which gives a (12,145,8) code (the A4(12,8) claim made at the same time), followed by deleting coordinate 0.
