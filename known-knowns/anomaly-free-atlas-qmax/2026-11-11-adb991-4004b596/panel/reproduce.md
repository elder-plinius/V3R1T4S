## 1. Own tools, written from CLAIM.md before reading acclist.c or mitm.c

- `mycheck.c` is a streaming checker. It enforces the exact line format: 15 integers written as `-?[1-9][0-9]*` or
  `0`, single spaces, a `\n` at the end of every line including the last, no blank lines, no `+`, no leading zeros,
  no `-0`. It also checks |F| <= Q_max, all six SM ACCs (Y^2 multiplied by 6), that each species is sorted ascending,
  that F >= canon(-F) lexicographically with canon(-F) = (-c,-b,-a) per species, gcd 1 (which implies nonzero), and
  that lines are strictly increasing. It reports the number of lines and the number of sign-self-conjugate lines,
  and exits nonzero on any failure. Negative tests (checker_strictness.txt, plus ad hoc tests): duplicate, altered,
  negated and doubled lines, a zero line, double, leading and trailing spaces, a tab, `+0`, `00`, a blank line, CRLF
  line ends and a missing final newline are all rejected.
- `mycount.c` is a counter that produces no list. Family-permutation orbits are counted directly as sorted triples
  (multisets), with no Burnside sum over S3^5. I derived the linear ACCs by hand: they force the species sums to be
  t*(1,-4,2,-3,6). linear_nullspace_check.txt confirms this by brute force over a box. For each t, the two largest
  species lists are joined in a hash keyed by the (quadratic, cubic) contributions, and the other three are looked
  up in it. gcd 1 is handled by Moebius inversion. The overall sign is handled by Burnside over Z2: the fixed points
  are {-a,0,a} in every species, and they satisfy only the Y F^2 condition. Count = 1 + (G + Gsym)/2. The split
  (largest two lists hashed) is different from both the lister's split (Q,u,d)|(L,e) and mitm.c's (Q,L,nu)|(u,d,e).
- `pylist.py` is a small-Q_max Python lister. It canonicalises each solution explicitly (gcd and sign checked per
  solution, no Moebius and no Burnside) and re-checks the linear ACCs with asserts.

## 4. Source reading: does the code implement the stated convention?

- acclist.c, MODE 0: species orbits are sorted triples. The species sums are taken from the linear ACCs: sd = -2sQ - su,
  sL = -3sQ, se = 2sQ - su, and SM forces su = -4sQ, which agrees with my t*(1,-4,2,-3,6). The (quadratic, cubic)
  keys use weights (1,-2,1,-1,1) and (6,3,3,2,1), matching CLAIM.md. Sign canonicalisation keeps F >= -F, decided at
  the first species whose triple differs from its negation; this is correct for lexicographic comparison of the
  per-species re-sorted tuples. Lines with gcd != 1 and the zero line are dropped. Output order is lexicographic by
  construction (Q, u and d iterated in lex order, and B entries sorted by key and then by lex index). This is the
  convention CLAIM.md states.
- mitm.c: it uses the same convention (multisets, Moebius over gcd, Z2 Burnside for the sign with {-a,0,a} fixed
  points, answer = 1 + (P + F)/2) and the split (Q,L,nu)|(u,d,e). CLAIM.md calls it a meet-in-the-middle count. It is
  not a Burnside sum over family permutations.

## Anything wrong or weak in the claim (none affects the numbers)

1. The claim's checkers do not enforce the exact line format that CLAIM.md gives as part of the canonical line.
   checklist.c and check_list.py accept double, leading and tab whitespace, blank lines, `+0`, `00` and a missing
   final newline (checker_strictness.txt: PASS on each of these mutations; mycheck fails all of them). The sha256
   pins the bytes, and my strict checker passed both full streams, so the certified lists are unaffected. But a PASS
   from the claim's checker alone does not certify the format.
2. Independence is mathematical, not methodological. mitm.c and my counter use the same decomposition (multiset
   orbits, Moebius, Z2 Burnside, a meet-in-the-middle on the quadratic and cubic keys). They differ in code and in
   split. The method-independent cross-checks are these:
   - the lister's direct enumeration, with explicit per-solution gcd and sign, gives line counts equal to the counters;
   - the explicit Python lister is byte-identical to acclist for Q_max <= 12;
   - the sign-symmetric line counts in the full lists equal the counters' Gsym.
3. Minor points:
   - acclist.c does not check fwrite errors, and it exits 0 even if a write fails. pipefail plus the downstream counts
     catch truncation.
   - REPRODUCE.md sends lister stderr to /dev/null.
   - The run times were 1249 s and 1529 s with both rows run in parallel, against the stated ~20 and ~24 min; the
     difference does not matter.
