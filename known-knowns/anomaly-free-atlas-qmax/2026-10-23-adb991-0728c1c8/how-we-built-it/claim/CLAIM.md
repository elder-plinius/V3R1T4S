# Claim

claim: In the convention of the Allanach-Davighi-Melville atlas, the number of inequivalent anomaly-free
family-dependent integer U(1) charge assignments of the Standard Model fermions plus three right-handed neutrinos
is 1643386480 for |F| <= 17 and 2720672222 for |F| <= 18, each certified by a complete canonical solution list
(count - 1 lines; the zero assignment is counted but not listed) identified by the sha256 of its uncompressed
stream in object/counts.txt.

target id: anomaly-free-atlas-qmax (charter quarry 5; not a bank entry)

## Criterion (verbatim, from the run's research charter, quarry 5)

"extend the exact census of anomaly-free U(1) charge assignments of the Standard Model fermions (with and without
three right-handed neutrinos) with integer charges bounded by `Q_max`, beyond the largest `Q_max` tabulated by
Allanach, Davighi and Melville; new exact counts, with the full solution list as the certificate."

Shape (charter): "record (exact counts past the published range) with a complete, independently recountable list."

## Definitions used

Charges F_X, X in {Q_i, u_i, d_i, L_i, e_i, nu_i}, i = 1, 2, 3, all-left-handed basis (Q:(3,2)_{1/6},
u^c:(3b,1)_{-2/3}, d^c:(3b,1)_{1/3}, L:(1,2)_{-1/2}, e^c:(1,1)_{1}, nu^c:(1,1)_{0}); sums over i:
  SU(3)^2 U(1)_F:  sum (2 F_Q + F_u + F_d) = 0
  SU(2)^2 U(1)_F:  sum (3 F_Q + F_L) = 0
  U(1)_Y^2 U(1)_F: sum (F_Q/6 + 4 F_u/3 + F_d/3 + F_L/2 + F_e) = 0
  grav^2 U(1)_F:   sum (6 F_Q + 3 F_u + 3 F_d + 2 F_L + F_e + F_nu) = 0
  U(1)_Y U(1)_F^2: sum (F_Q^2 - 2 F_u^2 + F_d^2 - F_L^2 + F_e^2) = 0
  U(1)_F^3:        sum (6 F_Q^3 + 3 F_u^3 + 3 F_d^3 + 2 F_L^3 + F_e^3 + F_nu^3) = 0
Flipping the sign of a whole species is a bijection of bounded integer solutions, so the count does not depend on
the left- or right-handed basis.

Convention ("inequivalent"): count = 1 + the number of nonzero integer solutions with |F| <= Q_max and gcd 1,
modulo independent permutations of the three families within each species and the overall sign F -> -F (the "1"
is the all-zero assignment). Not read from the paper (blocked here); fixed because it reproduces every published
value available (status.md).

## Context (not part of this claim)

Earlier rows of the same census in this workspace (Q_max 11..16 for SMnuR, 11..31 for SM) were panelled in other
runs; this claim is the two rows SMnuR Q_max = 17 and 18 of the same census table.

## Objects

- object/counts.txt: the count, the list line count and the sha256 of the uncompressed canonical list.
- The lists (1643386479 and 2720672221 lines) are regenerated by source/csenum2.c; they are never stored. Canonical line: species
  order Q u d L e nu; each species sorted ascending; of F and -F the lexicographically larger; gcd 1; nonzero;
  lines strictly increasing (lexicographic, as integer tuples); single spaces, "\n" line ends.
- source/count.c, count2.c (+ fix.py, conventions.py): counters without listing (raw count of all solutions,
  Burnside for the sign, Moebius for common factors).
