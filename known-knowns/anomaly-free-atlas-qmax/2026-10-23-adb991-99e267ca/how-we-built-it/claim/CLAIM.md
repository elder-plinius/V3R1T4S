# Claim

claim: With integer U(1) charges |F| <= 11, the number of inequivalent anomaly-free family-dependent charge
assignments is 37006 for the Standard Model fermion content (15 charges) and 45378378 for the Standard Model plus
three right-handed neutrinos (18 charges), counted in the convention of the Allanach-Davighi-Melville atlas.

target id: anomaly-free-atlas-qmax (charter quarry 5; not a bank entry)

## Criterion (verbatim, from the run's research charter, quarry 5)

"extend the exact census of anomaly-free U(1) charge assignments of the Standard Model fermions (with and without
three right-handed neutrinos) with integer charges bounded by `Q_max`, beyond the largest `Q_max` tabulated by
Allanach, Davighi and Melville; new exact counts, with the full solution list as the certificate."

Shape (charter): "record (exact counts past the published range) with a complete, independently recountable list."

## Definitions used

Charges F_X, X in {Q_i, u_i, d_i, L_i, e_i} (SM) or also nu_i (SMnuR), i = 1, 2, 3, all-left-handed basis
(Q:(3,2)_{1/6}, u^c:(3b,1)_{-2/3}, d^c:(3b,1)_{1/3}, L:(1,2)_{-1/2}, e^c:(1,1)_{1}, nu^c:(1,1)_{0}); sums over i:
  SU(3)^2 U(1)_F:  sum (2 F_Q + F_u + F_d) = 0
  SU(2)^2 U(1)_F:  sum (3 F_Q + F_L) = 0
  U(1)_Y^2 U(1)_F: sum (F_Q/6 + 4 F_u/3 + F_d/3 + F_L/2 + F_e) = 0
  grav^2 U(1)_F:   sum (6 F_Q + 3 F_u + 3 F_d + 2 F_L + F_e + F_nu) = 0
  U(1)_Y U(1)_F^2: sum (F_Q^2 - 2 F_u^2 + F_d^2 - F_L^2 + F_e^2) = 0
  U(1)_F^3:        sum (6 F_Q^3 + 3 F_u^3 + 3 F_d^3 + 2 F_L^3 + F_e^3 + F_nu^3) = 0
(SM: drop nu.) The source paper writes right-handed charges for u, d, e, nu; flipping the sign of a whole species
is a bijection of bounded integer solutions, so counts are basis-independent.

Convention ("inequivalent"): count = 1 + the number of nonzero integer solutions with |F| <= Q_max and gcd 1,
modulo independent permutations of the three families within each species and the overall sign F -> -F
(the "1" is the all-zero assignment). This convention was not read from the paper (blocked); it was fixed because
it reproduces every published value available (see status.md).

## Objects

- object/counts.txt: the two counts, list line counts and list hashes.
- object/SM_q11.txt.gz: the full SM list (37005 lines = 37006 - 1, canonical form below).
- The SMnuR list (45378377 lines, 417 MB gzip) is regenerated by source/acclist.c; its identity is the sha256 of
  the uncompressed stream in object/counts.txt.

Canonical form of a list line: species order Q u d L e [nu]; each species' three charges sorted ascending; of F
and -F the lexicographically larger; gcd 1; nonzero; lines in strictly increasing lexicographic order.
