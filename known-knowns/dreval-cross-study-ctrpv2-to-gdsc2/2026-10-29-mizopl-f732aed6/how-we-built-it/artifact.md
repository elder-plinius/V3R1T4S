### Largest Contributor Identification
1. **Largest Full-Dataset Contributor (All `40,292.4` Rows/Split): Mechanism `(iii)` (Response-Scale / Assay-Offset Calibration, fitted on GDSC2 labels).** Fitting a single additive offset $c_0 = \overline{y_{\text{true}} - \hat{y}}$ per split on GDSC2 labels lifts `SchurBilinearExVivoDRP` $R^2_{\text{norm}}$ by **`+0.42333`** (from `-0.42457` to `-0.00124 ± 0.00758`, closing **`99.71%`** of the negative $R^2_{\text{norm}}$ gap to zero and **`71.02%`** of the gap to in-study `CTRPv2 LCO`, while cutting MSE by `29.72%` from `6.38104` to `4.48488`, below `NaiveMeanEffectsPredictor` `5.25449`). Two-parameter linear OLS calibration (`c_0 + c_1 \hat{y}`, fitted on GDSC2 labels) lifts $R^2_{\text{norm}}$ by **`+0.43342`** to **`+0.00885 ± 0.00760`** (`102.09%` of gap to zero / `72.71%` of gap to `CTRPv2 LCO`), and 2-parameter affine residual calibration lifts $R^2_{\text{norm}}$ by **`+0.44239`** to **`+0.01781 ± 0.00353`** (`104.20%` of gap to zero / `74.21%` of gap to `CTRPv2 LCO`).
2. **Largest Label-Free Contributor (Zero GDSC2 Labels Used): Mechanism `(i)` (Concentration-Range Restriction / Window Alignment).** Because `CTRPv2` screens drugs up to a median maximum dose of `66.0 uM` ($\ln(66.0) = +4.19$) whereas `GDSC2` caps testing at a median maximum dose of `10.0 uM` ($\ln(10.0) = +2.30$), `CTRPv2`-trained models predict high $\ln(\text{IC}_{50})$ values above `GDSC2`'s upper screening limit where CurveCurator fits are extrapolated/censored. Restricting evaluation to in-window predictions $\ln(d_{\min}) \le \hat{y} \le \ln(d_{\max})$ (`Mechanism (i-a)`, `19,118.6` rows/split, using zero GDSC2 labels) lifts `SchurBilinearExVivoDRP` $R^2_{\text{norm}}$ by **`+0.46402`** to **`+0.03945 ± 0.01189`** (closing **`109.29%`** of the gap to zero and **`77.84%`** of the gap to `CTRPv2 LCO`), lifts $\text{Pearson}_{\text{norm}}$ from `+0.13294` to **`+0.36026`**, and cuts MSE by **`58.77%`** (`6.38104 -> 2.63072`).
3. **Negligible Contributor: Mechanism `(ii)` (Cell-Line Overlap Restriction).** Restricting evaluation to the `52–61` held-out CTRPv2-overlapping cell lines (`5,345.4` rows/split) vs the `375` disjoint GDSC2-only cell lines (`34,947.0` rows/split) changes $R^2_{\text{norm}}$ by only **`+0.01346`** (`-0.41111` vs `-0.42457`, closing just **`3.17%`** of the gap to zero and **`2.26%`** of the gap to `CTRPv2 LCO`).
