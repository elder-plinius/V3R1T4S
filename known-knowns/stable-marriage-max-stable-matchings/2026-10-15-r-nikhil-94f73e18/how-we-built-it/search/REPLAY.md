From how-we-built-it/, run python3 search/replay.py. It compiles the actual discovery source and replays each saved phase to the first proposal yielding its saved best. Only the wall-clock stop becomes a fixed proposal cutoff; mutation, validity, counting, RNG and acceptance code are unchanged. Every expected preference/word pair must match byte-for-byte. It also reruns the fresh random-start n7 calibration. No checkpoint reset occurs within either phase. The calibration starts before the first n19 phase according to the saved receipts.

After replaying the word search and fresh n7 calibration, the command exhausts the saved suffix family through depth four: first append a simple directed cycle of length at least three in the unused-pair graph, then permit cycles of length at least two. It reproduces all93 traversal nodes, with no time/node cutoff, and the selected JSON construction and preference bytes. Nodes may represent commuting duplicate profiles; the family is explicitly bounded by depth four.
