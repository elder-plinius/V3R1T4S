The default checker is the independent REPRODUCE reviewer's exact memoized binary-constraint checker, copied from review/independent.cpp and review/memo.cpp with one include spelling change: the standard unordered_map header uses quotes instead of angle brackets to avoid the kit placeholder scanner mistaking a standard header name for a template placeholder. The published panel describes and validates its recurrence: arc consistency safely removes unsupported values; after propagation, fixed variables may be removed; the remaining domain vector uniquely specifies the residual problem; branching partitions all completions; memoization reuses equal residual problems. It does not sample or truncate the count. This package adds only a wrapper for saved hashes, byte-exact reconstruction, positive/malformed controls and comparison with the supplied predecessor. The original author full leaf-by-leaf checker is unchanged and retained as run_full_checks.sh.
