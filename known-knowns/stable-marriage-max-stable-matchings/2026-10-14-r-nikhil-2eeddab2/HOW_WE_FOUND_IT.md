# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

The constructor lens of this run tried gluings of two order-7 record blocks for the untouched n = 14 cell. Two-copy gluings (Irving-Leather style doubling with the order-7 block as one half) gave exactly 2 x 85^2 = 14450. The lexicographic product O[K2], with the order-7 block as the OUTER structure and Knuth's order-2 instance inside (each person becomes a pair; men rank by the outer ranking, then by their inner list; women likewise with the inner order reversed), gave 24992 from Jo's (2026) order-7 instance with 85 stable matchings, and 23744 from the published order-7 instance with 81. Later checks showed this product is exactly the Irving-Leather doubling (it reproduces 268 and 195472 from Knuth's order-4 instance). A further 1.9 million exact evaluations of local search over the outer block, all 2^14 inner orientations, and about 3 million swap-move evaluations on the order-14 instance itself found nothing above 24992. Counts: a memoised rotation-poset DP, a lattice walk re-checking every matching, a reverse-search enumeration re-checking every matching, and a Python brute force. The run's PREREG.md froze the strict-improvement rule and the agreement-of-counters rule before any output was scored.
