# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

Warm-started iterated local search on an exact evaluator. The evaluator (smcount3.c) counts the stable matchings of an instance as the ideals of its rotation poset by a memoised dynamic programme, cross-checked on every new best by a lattice walk that re-checks each matching against the definition; it was validated on all published record instances and on 685 random and near-record instances against two independent counters. The search: random adjacent or arbitrary swaps in one preference list, sideways moves accepted, one or two forced random moves as a kick after 1000-3000 evaluations without improvement, restarts from the best; several books in parallel with different seeds, re-seeded from the best every 15-50 minutes. Starting from the published order-15 instance with 27059 stable matchings the books passed 27510, 28549, 29726, 30043 and reached 30362 after about 4 million exact evaluations; a further 2 million evaluations found nothing better. The run's PREREG.md froze the strict-improvement rule and the agreement-of-counters rule before any output was scored.
