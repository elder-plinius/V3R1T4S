# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

Warm-started iterated local search on an exact evaluator. The evaluator (smclimb.c, later smcount3.c) counts the stable matchings of an instance exactly (rotation-lattice walk, then a memoised rotation-poset ideal count cross-checked by a verified lattice walk); it was validated on all published record instances and on hundreds of random instances against two independent counters. The search: random adjacent or arbitrary swaps in one preference list, sideways moves accepted, a single forced random move as a kick after 1500 evaluations without improvement, restarts from the best. Starting from the published order-13 instance with 7123 stable matchings, one book reached 7415 after 75050 evaluations and 7698 after 91441 evaluations (about 5 minutes); roughly 12 million further evaluations with kicks of 1-3 moves and two annealing schedules found nothing better. The run's PREREG.md froze the strict-improvement rule and the agreement-of-counters rule before any output was scored.
