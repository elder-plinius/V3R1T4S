# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

The brief (BRIEF.md of the run) pointed at the five published record instances of Ong et al. (2025) as warm starts. A C evaluator (smclimb.c: exact count by a rotation-lattice walk, validated against two Python counters on all published records and on random instances) was wrapped in an iterated local search: random adjacent or arbitrary swaps in one preference list, sideways moves accepted, a single forced random move as a kick after 1000 evaluations without improvement, restarts from the best. Started from the published order-15 instance with 27059 stable matchings, one book found this 27510-matching instance after 1651 evaluations (28 seconds). The run's PREREG.md froze the bar (27059, strict) and the three-counter agreement rule before any search output was scored.
