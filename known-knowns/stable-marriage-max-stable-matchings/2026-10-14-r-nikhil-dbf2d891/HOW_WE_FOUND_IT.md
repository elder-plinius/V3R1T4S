Applying it twice to the order-7 instance with 85 stable matchings from M. Jo's Zenodo preprint of 2026-10-13 (the block itself re-counted here and by the panel over all 5040 matchings) gives this order-28 instance with 2497797760 stable matchings, counted by a memoised rotation-poset DP (2 s, about 2 GB) and by a reverse-search enumeration that re-checks every matching against the definition (about 45 minutes on one core; see RECEIPTS.md for the fast check and the panel files for the full enumerations). Doubling the status source's own published order-7 block (81) twice gives 2369365120, also above the table value; the claimed object is above that too.
