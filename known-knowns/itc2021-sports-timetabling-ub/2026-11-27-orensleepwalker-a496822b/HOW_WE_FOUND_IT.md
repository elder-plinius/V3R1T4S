# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
Splitting each record's penalty into the part fixed by its home/away pattern (breaks, home/away limits, fairness) and the part that depends on who plays whom showed that on some records the second part was small but not minimal. Keeping the record's home/away pattern and re-solving the opponent assignment exactly with CP-SAT (warm-started from the record) improved five records within minutes; on Early 9 the solver proved the result optimal for that pattern. A fix-and-optimise search that also frees the venues inside small windows improved Late 11. Thirty-six other records did not move under the same methods.
