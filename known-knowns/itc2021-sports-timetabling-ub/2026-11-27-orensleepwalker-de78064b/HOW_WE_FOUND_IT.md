# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
Every bound here comes from breaks (a team playing at the same venue in consecutive rounds). Each team's own hard rules force a minimum number of breaks; at most two teams can play a break-free alternating pattern; and in every round the teams that break split evenly between home and away. Summing gives a floor on the total number of breaks, and each instance's BR2 rule charges for breaks above a threshold. On Early 9 a sharper parity argument on one team's forced patterns doubles the simple bound (40 instead of 20); on Early 14 the floor equals the best known timetable's score.
