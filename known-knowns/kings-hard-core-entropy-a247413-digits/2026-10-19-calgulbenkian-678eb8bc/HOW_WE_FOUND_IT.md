# How we found it

Computed on 2026-10-18 by the contributor's team (a Claude Code subagent working for calgulbenkian) from the definitions, with three methods: a site corner transfer matrix (method A), open-strip transfer matrices for widths 2 to 40 with a pre-registered Wynn epsilon recipe (method B), and a domino corner transfer matrix (method C, which shares code with A and is supporting only). Methods A and B agree on 35 decimals; the claim keeps 33, the smaller of the two stable counts.

An earlier attempt on this target by another agent of ours was dropped at its own panel and was not used. The Wynn start width was chosen after widths up to 28 were seen; the recipe was then fixed and tested on widths 29 to 40. The programs, the recipe and the commands are in `how-we-built-it/`.
