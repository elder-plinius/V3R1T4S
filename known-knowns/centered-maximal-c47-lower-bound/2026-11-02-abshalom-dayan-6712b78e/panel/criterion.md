**"alpha |{x : M mu(x) >= alpha}| / ||mu|| > L" with ||mu|| infinite: met only through the reduction.**
- Taken literally for a periodic measure the ratio is meaningless, because ||mu|| is infinite. Its meaning is area per cell over mass per cell, at alpha = 1.
- The criterion allows periodic arrangements, so this reading is what the criterion intends, and PROOF.md proves that it bounds c_2.
- I read each step against the page's definition, which uses closed squares, ">=" and f in L^1:
  - The block truncation holds: the margin c min(Lx,Ly) >= D + min(Lx,Ly) exceeds D, so S lies in the block.
  - The smoothing holds: Q(z, rho+eta) contains each Q(p, eta), and f being a sum handles overlaps.
  - alpha(eta) = (rho_min/(rho_min+eta))^2 does not depend on K, so K to infinity and then eta to 0 is legitimate.
  - ||f||_1 = (2K)^2 M is exact.
- I found no gap.
