# How We Found It

contributor text, unverified

Existing MOABB SSVEP pipelines fail on at least one of the three `SSVEP (All classes)` datasets for structural signal-processing reasons:
1. **Riemannian filter-bank pipelines (`SSVEP_MDM`, `SSVEP_TS+LR`)** bandpass-filter only around the fundamental frequencies $[f_k - 0.5, f_k + 0.5]\text{ Hz}$, discarding the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th SSVEP harmonics that carry substantial visual cortex SNR and discarding phase-locked temporal correlation (`Nakanishi2015`).
2. **Time-locked template/reference methods (`SSVEP_CCA`, `SSVEP_TRCA`, `SSVEP_MsetCCA`)** have no supervised classifier head (picking $\arg\max_f r_1(f)$ blindly, failing when `Kalunga2016` includes a non-flickering `rest` class) and fail on `MAMEM3` (`23.70%` for `TRCA`, `13.80%` for `CCA`) because all 5 stimulus boxes flicker continuously while an arrow cues the target, so stimulus phase is not locked across trials and 10 of the 14 Emotiv EPOC channels are noisy non-occipital channels.

We designed `FBHTRSSVEPClassifier` (`how-we-built-it/fb_htr_ssvep.py`), a single fixed sklearn-compatible estimator (subclassing `SSVEP_CCA` so MOABB's `_pipeline_requires_epochs` automatically passes `mne.Epochs` under `WithinSessionEvaluation(paradigm=SSVEP(), random_state=42)`) that fuses three complementary views strictly fitted inside each training fold:
1. **Parseval Multi-Harmonic Log-Euclidean Tangent Space (`ts` view, weight `0.15`, $C=0.1$)**: Computes narrowband cross-spectral covariance matrices across fundamental $[f_k - 0.6, f_k + 0.6]$, 2nd harmonic $[2f_k - 0.8, 2f_k + 0.8]$, 3rd harmonic $[3f_k - 1.0, 3f_k + 1.0]$, and broadband $[7, 45]\text{ Hz}$ directly in the `rfft` domain via Parseval's identity, regularizes via Oracle Approximating Shrinkage (OAS) with trace floor `0.03`, trace-normalizes, and maps via matrix logarithm $\log(\Sigma)$ into Euclidean tangent space.
2. **Multi-Subband QR-SVD Canonical Correlation & Quadrature Energy (`cca` view, weight `0.40`, $C=0.2$)**: Extracts whitened SVD canonical correlations ($r_1, r_1^2, r_2$, and across-frequency z-scores) against 4-harmonic sinusoidal bases across 4 filter-bank sub-bands and 3 spatial whitening regularizations ($\lambda \in \{10^{-4}, 10^{-2}, 10^{-1}\}$), combined with phase-invariant per-channel quadrature harmonic energy $\log \|(X Q_{f,h})_c\|_2^2$, across-frequency SNR, leave-one-out debiased time-locked template spatial filters, and phase-invariant Harmonic Spatio-Spectral Decomposition (SSD) generalized eigenvalue filters ($R_f w = \lambda S_{\text{tot}} w$) learned strictly on the training fold.
3. **Joint Concatenated View (`joint` view, weight `0.45`, $C=0.1$)**: Concatenates standardized tangent-space and spectral-correlation/SSD features and solves the exact dual L2-regularized `LogisticRegression` via Representer-Theorem Gram reduction ($G = F_{\text{tr}} F_{\text{tr}}^\top$). Across all 32 subject-sessions, the BAN Bradley-Terry MLE tournament assigns `FB_HTR_SSVEP` dominance weight $\pi = 0.9097$ vs `SSVEP_TS+LR` $\pi = 0.0645$ and `SSVEP_MDM` $\pi = 0.0257$.
