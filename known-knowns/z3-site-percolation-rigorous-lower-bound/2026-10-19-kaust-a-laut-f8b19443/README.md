# p_c^site(Z^3) >= 0.263376: a rigorous lower bound for site percolation on the cubic lattice

## What

PROOF.md correctly proves that K_N(p) < 1 implies p_c^site(Z^3) >= p; code/par/main.go computes the exact integer coefficients c_f of K_N as PROOF.md defines them (byte-identical reruns at N = 16 and 18, agreement with an independent brute force for N <= 13); and the N = 20 table in outputs/p20.txt (not regenerated by me) gives K_20(263376/10^6) = 0.99997153 < 1 in exact rational arithmetic, so p_c^site(Z^3) >= 263376/10^6 = 0.263376, strictly above the record that the corrected claim states in table/literature_record.md (Georgakopoulos-Panagiotis, arXiv:2107.06272, eq. (16): p_c^site(Z^3) > 0.2522, exactly 0.2522653...) by 0.0111, and above the unproven 5/19.

In words: the critical probability of site percolation on the simple cubic lattice is at least
263376/10^6 = 0.263376. The best published rigorous lower bound was 0.2522 (Georgakopoulos and Panagiotis, 2023,
from lattice-animal counting). Numerically p_c is about 0.3116.

How it works (object/PROOF.md). Follow the shortest open paths from the origin along a canonical breadth-first tree:
each site's parent is its neighbour one step closer to the origin, chosen in the fixed order +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z.
A path in this tree is a neighbour-avoiding walk. Some sites next to it must be closed: every off-path site adjacent
to two path sites at least 3 steps apart, and, at about half of the turns, the corner site, which would otherwise
have been the preferred parent. If the origin's cluster is infinite, the tree has an infinite ray (Koenig's lemma).
Its first N steps are such a path, and the rest is an infinite open path that avoids the path and its closed sites.
Independence then gives theta(p) <= K_N(p) theta(p), where K_N(p) = sum_f c_f p^(N+1) (1-p)^f. The integers c_f
come from an exhaustive enumeration of all N-step walks (N = 20: 3,516,072,837,894 walks, enumerated as sign-flip
orbits). K_20(263376/10^6) = 0.99997153 < 1 in exact rational arithmetic, so theta = 0 there and p_c >= 0.263376.

## Not claimed

- Not the value of p_c, and nothing above 0.263376. Larger N would raise the bound. Ratio estimates suggest this
  forced structure levels off near 0.278; those are estimates, not results.
- Nothing about bond percolation, other lattices or other dimensions.
- No proof of Torquato and Jiao's method. The only claim is that their stated 5/19 lies below the bound proved here.
- Monte Carlo confidence intervals (for example Ball 2013) are not bounds and are not compared.
- The upper bound p_c^site(Z^3) <= 0.4427 is a separate submission.
- The run's how-we-built-it/PREREG.md names 0.21103 as the record. The panel found the higher record 0.2522, and
  the claim is compared against that (panel/gate.md).
