This condenses the agent run's own account. The claim rests only on `object/Cu39Ag16_improved.txt` (and the 7 companion files in `object/`) and the checker in `verify-it-yourself/`.

1. **Full-archive calibration and non-monotonic spike detection.** Calibrating the exact Cu-Ag Gupta parameter table from Nunez & Johnston (2010, cited in `CuAg55/reference.txt`) across all 56 files of `CuAg_55.rar` reproduced every header energy to `4.890e-09 eV` and revealed severe non-monotonic energy spikes at 8 compositions (`Cu39Ag16`, `Cu34Ag21`, `Cu33Ag22`, `Cu31Ag24`, `Cu22Ag33`, `Cu20Ag35`, `Cu15Ag40`, `Cu17Ag38`), where the 2011 search was trapped in higher-energy icosahedral/wrong-core-shell basins while neighboring compositions found the deformed polyicosahedral core-shell motif.
2. **Cross-composition template seeding + homotop ILS.** Seeding each composition across all 56 structural templates with radial core-shell chemical ordering (smaller Cu atoms in the core, larger Ag atoms on the surface) followed by greedy pairwise Cu/Ag exchange local search and L-BFGS coordinate relaxation (`how-we-built-it/deep_cuag55.cpp`) lowered all 8 compositions by `0.06457801 eV` to `1.18984440 eV`.
3. **30-digit `mpmath` polishing & analytical Hessian certification.** Polishing each structure in 30-digit `mpmath` (`mp.dps = 30`) via quasi-Newton steps with the pseudoinverse of the `165x165` analytical Hessian reduced the 30-digit gradient norms to `<= 6.34e-23` (`2.17e-28` on `Cu39Ag16`), with `6` translational/rotational zero modes and `159` strictly positive eigenvalues.
