# f(2,5) = 14: no two tournaments on 13 vertices have every 5-subset dominated

## What

Partial win p1 for f(2,5), not the full success criterion: the CNF regenerated by verify-it-yourself/encode_schutte.py 2 5 13 --sb global3 (sha256 247ca74489c8144e2eca1fa1b7bfdd6c2f7f826e7deac2c46e4b3748c7069626, 42644 variables, 148931 clauses) is satisfiable whenever two tournaments on 13 vertices have property S_5 of Definitions 2.1 and 2.2 of arXiv:2604.08790 (base clauses and every symmetry-breaking clause checked by the CRITERION and REPRODUCE skeptics); cake_lpr (commit a36874a8b750b43fe4b385b8ddbf5b033e46a3fa) printed "s VERIFIED UNSAT" for this CNF and the CaDiCaL 3.0.1 LRAT proof with sha256 d87598122cfb5f17397537deb15b893d7aaae49598a81ce41a1c92191d0de99a (371954317 bytes) in the contributor's runs (DmitrL-dev, AI-assisted) of 2026-10-28, 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-22, and for this CNF and proofs it generated itself in the REPRODUCE skeptic's runs of 2026-10-28; with the one-vertex extension and the published 14-vertex composition of two order-7 Paley tournaments this gives f(2,5) = 14.

The certificate is the CNF named by object/cnf_spec.json (generated by verify-it-yourself/encode_schutte.py with
`2 5 13 --sb global3`; 42644 variables, 148931 clauses, sha256 247ca74489c8144e2eca1fa1b7bfdd6c2f7f826e7deac2c46e4b3748c7069626)
and the LRAT proof that CaDiCaL 3.0.1 writes for it; cake_lpr, the CakeML-verified checker, accepts that proof
(`s VERIFIED UNSAT`). The exact CNF ships as object/m2k5n13_global3.cnf.gz (489515 bytes, sha256 96ce1693fbd585372355cc986fcc99fa32729c15555ca8828a5ac505074fdfc7); `gunzip -c` gives the
2116234-byte DIMACS file with sha256 247ca74489c8144e2eca1fa1b7bfdd6c2f7f826e7deac2c46e4b3748c7069626, every symmetry-breaking clause included. The
LRAT proof (371954317 bytes, sha256 d87598122cfb5f17397537deb15b893d7aaae49598a81ce41a1c92191d0de99a) is not stored: an upload of the 372 MB file to
a public archive did not complete. CaDiCaL 3.0.1 at the pinned commit writes this exact proof again from that CNF with
`cadical --lrat --no-binary -t 3600 m2k5n13_global3.cnf m2k5n13_global3.lrat` (byte-identical in the contributor's runs
of 2026-10-28, 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-22 and in the REPRODUCE skeptic's run C, all on Apple Silicon). Another platform
or build may write a different proof; any proof that cake_lpr accepts for this CNF is a certificate.
The encoding and the symmetry breaking are argued in how-we-built-it/SOUNDNESS.md, which also gives the clause-index
range of every clause family in this CNF. The upper bound
is the published construction (object/upper_bound_m2k5n14.json, Proposition 2.3 with two order-7 Paley tournaments).
This is partial win p1 of the bank entry (an exact value), not the main criterion (no smaller construction is claimed).

## Check it yourself

```sh
sh verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh
```

Expected last line: `PASS`. About 5 minutes on an Apple M-series laptop (about 100 s if CADICAL and CAKE_LPR point to
already built tools); needs python3 (standard library), git, make, a C compiler, network access to github.com (it builds
CaDiCaL and cake_lpr at pinned commits), about 1.6 GB of free memory and 0.5 GB of disk. The script regenerates the CNF
(2.1 MB, also shipped as object/m2k5n13_global3.cnf.gz) and the proof (372 MB, not stored); RUN_IT_YOURSELF.md shows how
to check the shipped CNF with cake_lpr directly. Details: RUN_IT_YOURSELF.md; real output: verify-it-yourself/RECEIPTS.md.

## Not claimed

- No construction below the composition bars: the main criterion (f(2,5) <= 13 or f(3,6) <= 12) is not met, and this
  result shows the first half is impossible.
- Nothing about f(3,6). The same encoding for three tournaments on 12 vertices is UNSAT according to CaDiCaL (7089 s), but
  its LRAT proof exhausted cake_lpr's heap at 1500 and 2600 MB on the contributor's laptop, so there is no verified
  certificate yet; it was not reviewed by a panel.
- verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh audits the base clauses of the CNF mechanically (cnf_audit.py); the added
  (symmetry-breaking) clauses are checked by the argument in how-we-built-it/SOUNDNESS.md, which the REPRODUCE panelist
  re-derived and re-implemented independently, and by the randomised test how-we-built-it/sb_audit.py.
- Like the paper (proof of Proposition 2.2), the lower bound uses the convention that a vertex set of size at most k is
  not S_k (the bank's n >= k+1).
- Nothing about physical dice realising the tournaments.
