# f(2,5) = 14: no two tournaments on 13 vertices have every 5-subset dominated

## What

Partial win p1 for f(2,5), not the full success criterion: the CNF regenerated by verify-it-yourself/encode_schutte.py 2 5 13 --sb global3 (sha256 247ca74489c8144e2eca1fa1b7bfdd6c2f7f826e7deac2c46e4b3748c7069626, 42644 variables, 148931 clauses) is satisfiable whenever two tournaments on 13 vertices have property S_5 of Definitions 2.1 and 2.2 of arXiv:2604.08790 (base clauses and every symmetry-breaking clause checked in this review), so cake_lpr's acceptance of an UNSAT proof for exactly this CNF, which this review did not run, together with the one-vertex extension and the published 14-vertex composition of two order-7 Paley tournaments, gives f(2,5) = 14.

The certificate is the CNF named by object/cnf_spec.json (generated by verify-it-yourself/encode_schutte.py with
`2 5 13 --sb global3`; 42644 variables, 148931 clauses, sha256 247ca74489c8144e2eca1fa1b7bfdd6c2f7f826e7deac2c46e4b3748c7069626)
and the LRAT proof that CaDiCaL 3.0.1 writes for it; cake_lpr, the CakeML-verified checker, accepts that proof
(`s VERIFIED UNSAT`). The encoding and the symmetry breaking are argued in how-we-built-it/SOUNDNESS.md. The upper bound
is the published construction (object/upper_bound_m2k5n14.json, Proposition 2.3 with two order-7 Paley tournaments).
This is partial win p1 of the bank entry (an exact value), not the main criterion (no smaller construction is claimed).

Checks: the contributor's session ran verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh (verify-it-yourself/RECEIPTS.md); the REPRODUCE
panelist wrote its own checker and its own CNF from Definition 2.1, matched the shipped CNF clause for clause, re-derived
the soundness of every group of added clauses, built CaDiCaL and cake_lpr at the pinned commits and reproduced the UNSAT
answer and the cake_lpr verdict on both CNFs (panel/reproduce.md); the NOVELTY panelist checked the status page, the
author's dissertation, the last 24 months of literature and this repository (panel/novelty.md); the CRITERION panelist
judged the claim against the criterion and p1 (panel/criterion.md).

## Not claimed

- No construction below the composition bars: the main criterion (f(2,5) <= 13 or f(3,6) <= 12) is not met, and this
  result shows the first half is impossible.
- Nothing about f(3,6). The same encoding for three tournaments on 12 vertices is UNSAT according to CaDiCaL (7089 s), but
  its LRAT proof exhausted cake_lpr's heap at 1500 and 2600 MB on the contributor's laptop, so there is no verified
  certificate yet; it was not reviewed by a panel.
- verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh audits the base clauses of the CNF mechanically (cnf_audit.py); the added
  (symmetry-breaking) clauses are checked by the argument in how-we-built-it/SOUNDNESS.md, which the REPRODUCE panelist
  re-derived and re-implemented independently, and by the randomised test how-we-built-it/sb_audit.py.
- Like the paper (proof of Proposition 2.2), the lower bound uses the convention that a vertex set of size at most k is
  not S_k (the bank's n >= k+1).
- Nothing about physical dice realising the tournaments.
