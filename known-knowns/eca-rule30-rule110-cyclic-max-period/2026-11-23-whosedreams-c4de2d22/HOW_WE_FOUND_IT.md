# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

Brief: compute the exact maximum eventual period of elementary CA Rule 30 (A334497) on a ring of 37 cells, by an exhaustive search with two independent programs that agree. This is the Rule 30 sibling of the Rule 110 width-37 claim A204371(37)=4403 (V3R1T4S PR #1173), and it uses the same two programs, byte-identical.

What was prepared first: the plan, how-we-built-it/PREREG.md, was written and frozen on 2026-11-22T16:51:29Z (11:51 ET), before anyone on this project had run Rule 30 at width 37. It fixes the programs (by sha256), the self-test, an independent witness checker (how-we-built-it/check_period.py, written for this run from the definition), the floor A334496(37)=49276415, a held-out width, a live-table check, and the stopping rules. A small run kit (how-we-built-it/run_r30.sh with build.sh and status.sh) ran all stages in order on the contributor's PC, and was smoke-tested at widths up to 20 on the packaging machine first.

What happened: on an AMD Ryzen 9 9900X with about 55 GiB visible to WSL2, under nice -n 19, the self-test (2026-11-22 13:06 to 13:15 ET) matched A334497(1..24) and A204371(1..30) exactly for both programs (108 OK lines) and both reported A334497(25..30) equal to the listed terms. The held-out stage (13:15 to 13:24 ET) gave A204371(31)=2759 and A334497(31)=2841150 from both programs, matching OEIS. At width 37, impl1 ran from 13:24 to 14:26 ET (3739.3822 s, peak RSS 16791800 kB) and impl2 from 14:26 ET to 01:51 ET on 2026-11-23 (41058.0073 s, peak RSS 33943584 kB). Both reported max_period=49276415 with witness 0x7fbfe703e, and check_period.py confirmed that period for both witnesses (about 21 s each). Nothing failed or was restarted; all logs are in how-we-built-it/logs/, how-we-built-it/selftest/, how-we-built-it/heldout/ and how-we-built-it/results/.

A later check on the packaging machine, not part of the original search, re-iterated the witness with a separate C iterator and also started from a single live cell: the witness is the first state of the single-cell cycle (transient 260564, period 49276415). So the maximum is the single-cell floor A334496(37), and the exhaustive census is the evidence that no longer cycle exists. While packaging we re-opened Wolfram (1986): Table 9.2 lists 49276415 for N=37 from sampled seeds (the plan had not re-opened that paper; PREREG check 4 says a published sampled value must be met or explained). The result meets it with equality, so the census turns that 1986 lower bound into the exact value. An earlier rough estimate for the whole run was 8 to 14 hours; it took about 12 h 45 min.
