## Definition (Sec. 1.5)

(n, d, w)_4 DNA code: words over {A, C, G, T} of length n with GC weight w (here w = floor(n/2)); d_edit(x, y) >= d for
distinct codewords; d_edit(x^RC, y) >= d for all codewords x, y "including x = y". Edit distance = Levenshtein
(substitutions, insertions, deletions). E_4^{RC,GC}(n, d, w) is the maximum size. Sec. 1.5 states that forbidden-
subword, consecutive-bases and frame-shift constraints "will not be discussed in this thesis".
