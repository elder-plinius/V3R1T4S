1. **Complete 2,192-Row IAU MDC Augmentation**: To avoid the classic trap where a candidate cluster matches an obscure Working List, Pending, or Removed MDC entry (or one of the `193` legacy MDC rows that list geocentric radiant $(\lambda_\odot, \alpha_g, \delta_g, V_g)$ without heliocentric $(q, e, i, \Omega, \omega)$), we first built a calibrated exact Southworth–Hawkins $D_{\text{SH}}$ oracle (`how-we-built-it/check_meteor_cluster.py`) and derived exact Keplerian orbits for all `172` radiant-only MDC rows with valid $V_g$.
2. **CLSS Stage II–IV Metric-Ball Extraction & Seam Surgery (`how-we-built-it/discover_new_showers.py`)**: Across `1,772,720` multi-station (`Num stat >= 3`) GMN trajectories from `2018–2026`, we isolated `193,724` high-quality unassigned (`IAU No == -1`) trajectories separated by $D_{\text{SH}} \ge 0.25$ and radiant separation $> 8^\circ$ from all `2,192` MDC entries. Iterative 5D $D_{\text{SH}}$ metric-ball extraction and boundary seam pruning isolated four multi-year Main clusters (`M2_Hercules` $N=20$, `M1_Cepheus_Core` $N=15$, `M1_Cepheus_MC` $N=15$ with 100% per-meteor $\ge 2$ countries, and `M3_Camelopardalis` $N=16$) and a single-night `4.84`-hour Cygnus–Draco outburst on `2025-08-31/09-01` (`p1`, $N=10$, $\max D_{\text{SH}} = 0.0619 < 0.08$).
3. **Working-List Multi-Year Recurrence Audit (`how-we-built-it/audit_p2_and_independent_search.py`)**: Template-matching all `36` strictly single-year `status == 0` Working List showers in `streamfulldata2026.txt` against multi-station GMN orbits across `2019–2026` certified `17` showers recurring in $3\text{--}6$ distinct GMN years with $D_{\text{SH}} < 0.06 < 0.10$ (`p2`).
