**In plain language:** Standard phenotype-driven rare-disease tools (`LIRICAL`, `Phenomizer`) compare a patient's observed Human Phenotype Ontology (`HPO`) symptoms against curated human disease-gene annotations (`HPOA`). When a patient carries a causal variant in a human gene with **zero** `HPO` annotations in the pinned knowledge base (`127` `P2-Raw` cases across `10` completely unannotated genes in `HPOA v2026-06-06`, and `988` blind `P2-Holdout` cases across `112` genes once circular case-report PMIDs are removed), direct symptom-to-gene matching has nothing to match and leaves the causal gene tied near rank `12,000` of `19,296` protein-coding genes (`MRR = 0.000083`, `0%` Top-100 recall). `MARG-P2` (`object/marg_p2_ranker.py`) infers unannotated human disease genes through strictly pre-corpus (`< 2026-06-09`), literature-textmining-free biological routes — mammalian/vertebrate orthologue knockout phenotypes bridged via `uPheno` OWL equivalences (`f1`), zero-diagonal L2-row-whitened physical/functional propagation over `STRING v12.0` (`textmining = 0`, `f2`), and specific `Gene Ontology` + `HGNC` macromolecular module transfer (`L_1` consensus `f3` + `L_3` paralogue-peak `f4` + 2nd-order cross-species-to-complex bridge `f5` + bounded log-concordance `f6`), scoring all `19,296` candidate genes symmetrically.

## Not claimed

1. **Milestone `p2` Partial Win Only (`Main Goal`, `p1`, and `p3` Not Claimed):** This package addresses **Milestone `p2`** exclusively (ranking causal genes with zero HPO annotations in the pinned HPOA release over all `19,296` protein-coding genes). It does **not** claim the full-corpus `Main Goal` (see open `elder-plinius/V3R1T4S` `PR #1068` on `arXiv:2609.02473`), `p1` (`<= 3` HPO terms, open `PR #1064`), or `p3` (temporal leakage audit, open `PR #1058`).
2. **`P2-Raw` Pleiotropic NDD Cohort Skew, `Top-1` / `Top-10` Ceiling & Negative-Control Behavior:** Three neurodevelopmental delay genes (`U2AF2`: `48`, `MAPK8IP3`: `20`, `EZH1`: `19`) account for `87 / 127` (`68.5%`) of `P2-Raw` cases. Without patient exome VCFs, `MARG-P2` lifts `MRR` `13.2x` over `LIRICAL 2.4.1` (`0.001089` vs `0.000083`, disjoint across case, gene-cluster, and publication-cluster 95% CIs) and lowers median rank on `9 / 10` genes (`8957.0` vs `12109.0`), while achieving `Top-1 = 0 / 127` and `Top-10 = 0 / 127` (`Top-50 = 2 / 127`; standalone `Route 1` `f1` reaches `Top-10 = 4 / 127` [`0.0315`], `MRR = 0.004187`). Because `68.5%` of `P2-Raw` cases share broad neurodevelopmental phenotypes across `U2AF2`/`MAPK8IP3`/`EZH1`, permuting labels *within* `P2-Raw` (`case MRR = 0.000366`, 95% case CI `[0.000169, 0.000705]`) overlaps `MARG-P2` in `case MRR` CI while `macro-gene MRR` drops `22.1x` (`0.004329` vs `0.000196`); meanwhile, both the analytical uniform `19,296` null (`0.000541`) and the `988`-case `P2-Holdout` shuffled-gene control (`0.000991`, 95% case CI `[0.000436, 0.001750]` vs `MARG-P2` `[0.004710, 0.013741]`) separate cleanly.
3. **Two Total Blind-Split Evaluation Passes (`Pass 1` vs. `Pass 2`):** As documented in `how-we-built-it/PREREG.md`, `total_blind_test_evaluations = 2` (`Pass 1` pre-audit + `Pass 2` after replacing post-`2026-06-09` `mim2gene_medgen` and `en_product6.xml` strictly with `genes_to_disease_v2026-06-06.txt` with zero formula or hyperparameter changes).
4. **Phenotype-Only Scope & Local Panel Status:** Phenotype-only ranking narrows `19,296` genes without replacing variant-level exome interpretation (`Exomiser` / `LIRICAL --vcf`).
