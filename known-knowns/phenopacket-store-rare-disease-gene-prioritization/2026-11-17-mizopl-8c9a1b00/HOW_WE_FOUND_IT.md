1. **Strict `< 2026-06-09` Pre-Corpus Stratification & Leakage Audit:** Parsing all `10,377` phenopackets against the `19,296` HGNC protein-coding gene space (`2026-05-01` monthly snapshot, verified `max date_modified == 2026-05-01`) and pre-corpus `HPO/HPOA v2026-06-06` (`2026-06-08`, deriving all `7,653` OMIM and `8,288` Orphanet links strictly from `genes_to_disease.txt`) isolated `127` `P2-Raw` cases (`10` causal genes) with zero HPO annotations even in untouched `HPOA v2026-06-06`, plus `2,120` corpus-only cases (`224` causal genes) whose sole HPOA rows cite the `1,755` corpus case-report PMIDs. Splitting those `224` sorted corpus-only genes deterministically into `P2-Dev` (`112` even-indexed genes, `1,132` cases) and blind `P2-Holdout` (`112` odd-indexed genes, `988` cases) provided a gene-disjoint development ground (`see how-we-built-it/PREREG.md` for full accounting of Pass 1 vs. Pass 2 after purging post-cutoff `mim2gene_medgen` and `en_product6.xml` with zero formula or hyperparameter changes).
2. **Diagnosing Two Failure Modes on `P2-Dev`:** On `P2-Dev`, standalone cross-species `Monarch KG 2026-05-09` + `Upheno v2025-10-12` (`upheno-equivalence-model.obo`, `11,838` `HP <-> MP` subsumption + `1,954` equivalence bridges) reached `MRR = 0.008235` (`Top-10 = 0.0177`), limited on essential multi-subunit complexes whose homozygous mouse knockouts arrest at embryonic lethality. Conversely, naive Random-Walk-with-Restart on `STRING v12.0` (`textmining = 0`) collapsed onto high-degree hubs (`VIRMA` ranked `#1` on `829 / 1,132` cases, `MRR = 0.000170`). Zero-diagonal L2-row-norm whitening plus `L_1` multi-subunit consensus and `L_3` single-paralogue peak pooling across `STRING-NoTM`, `GO 2026-05-19`, and `HGNC` gene groups eliminated hub collapse (`macro-gene MRR = 0.023625`).
