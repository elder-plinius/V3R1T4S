## Scope: what is and is not independently verified here
Verified exactly by this program: items 1-6 above, with labelling conventions (vertex numbering, fold-index numbering, witness field roles, tree encoding) recovered from the paper + data. Soundness does not depend on those labels: every accepted record proves its own mathematical statement for the stated (edge, vertex, inequality, cover); a mislabelled but accepted record would still eliminate its box.
NOT re-proved here (taken from the paper, listed under "what the proof rests on"): the reduction proposition (every configuration has a representative in D, i.e. the symmetry argument itself; only its finite checks in appendix A were redone), the centering lemma, the zoom lemma and coverage lemmas (tube, point, hand-over) as mathematics, the no-fit-set lemmas beyond the exact finite checks done (aligned: r=0; arc plane: support value check), continuity / Bernstein lemma. The header `policy` hash is not interpreted. The Local covered sets and parameters are checked against the paper text, not against Hervay's source.
Convention notes for the main session: the checksum is over the JSON text only (not including the space or newline). Vertex indices are the positions in the exact-coordinate sorted list (pair-lexicographic); the antipodal numbering also accepts all Global records (sec. 6 symmetry) but rejects the covers. For witnesses `{direction, contact, vertex}`: contact = hole vertex on the supporting line, vertex = plug vertex (the opposite of the naive reading).
