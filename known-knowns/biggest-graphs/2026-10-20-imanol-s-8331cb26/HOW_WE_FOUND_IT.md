# How we found it

Contributor text, unverified by the mechanical checker.

A facility-location exchange search found 14 radius-3 balls covering the published 8424-vertex baseline. The initial constructor scored cuts before adding the new vertex and failed. Scoring the edge cuts with all new-vertex links present reduced the exact defect to 16; two unchanged annealing chunks reduced it to zero. No rejected cross-proposal distance caching was integrated. Explicit objects, not stochastic superiority, are the claim. The main checker and three fresh independent checkers then ran all roots. The (15,3) alternatives tested during this run failed and are not packaged here.
