# How we found it

Found on 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-19 by the contributor's team (an AI search agent, Gemini "Argon"). A seed from a bordered Toeplitz double-circulant Hermitian self-orthogonal code over GF(4) had distance 4 with two stray weight-4 words; a climb by weight-4 Hermitian transvections (which keep self-orthogonality and rank) removed them, giving a pure [[23,9,5]] code. The programs are in `how-we-built-it/`; seeds and random state are not recorded.
