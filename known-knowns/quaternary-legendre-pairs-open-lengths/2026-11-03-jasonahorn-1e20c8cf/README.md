# Empty order-12 multiplier families at length 70

## What

There is no quaternary Legendre pair of length 70 in which both sequences are constant on the orbits of (1, 3, 9, 11, 13, 17, 27, 29, 33, 39, 47, 51) or both are constant on the orbits of (1, 9, 11, 23, 29, 37, 39, 43, 51, 53, 57, 67), the two order-12 subgroups of (Z/70Z)* that do not contain -1.

## Check it yourself

From this folder: `sh verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh`

Expected last line: PASS

Rough laptop time: one minute.

## Compared to

Status page https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25573, fetched 2026-11-03T07:02:12Z, HTTP 200. Harshit Verma gives a quaternary Legendre pair of length 64 and does not list a pair of length 70. Lebedev, arXiv:2609.04589, exhausted a reversible factor-2 queue at length 70 (170/170 ranks, 39798 hits, no pair) and said that is not nonexistence.

## Not claimed

No pair of length 70. No nonexistence proof. The order-6 group of 18 orbits and the CRT exponent family are outside this sentence, even where a separate scan was empty. Groups that contain -1 are outside this sentence.
