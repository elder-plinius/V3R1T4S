# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
An evaluator written from the HSEval specification split each record's cost by constraint. AU-BG-98's 128 was mostly split teacher assignments (110 of 128): courses taught by different teachers across their lessons. A CP-SAT large-neighbourhood search that re-solves the times and teachers of whole courses jointly, with room capacity handled by counting bounds instead of room variables, unified four split courses (110 -> 70) and reached 91. On the Danish instances, room pools that are nested or identical were compressed into one capacity per time slot, which made windows of 45-80 lessons solvable to optimality; after many equal-cost moves, one window lowered DK-VG-09's objective by 1 and another lowered DK-HG-12's by 4, both at the record's infeasibility.
