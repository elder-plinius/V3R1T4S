## 1. Diagnosing the Three Structural Seams in Standard MoleculeACE Baselines

Auditing the official MoleculeACE baseline implementation (`MoleculeACE/benchmark/featurization.py` and `the-record-we-beat/MoleculeACE_results.csv`, van Tilborg et al., *JCIM* 2022) revealed three structural bottlenecks that cause all 24 standard baselines (and even 80M-molecule pre-trained foundation models) to degrade sharply on activity-cliff test molecules (`cliff_mol == 1`):
1. **Achiral Fingerprint Blindness:** `Featurizer.ecfp` calls `AllChem.GetMorganFingerprintAsBitVect(mol, radius=2, nBits=1024)` without `useChirality=True`. On stereocenter-rich targets such as `CHEMBL287_Ki` (Sigma intracellular receptor $\sigma_1$, where `40.4%` of molecules contain `R/S` tetrahedral stereocenters and `1.5%` contain `E/Z` stereobonds), enantiomeric pairs with $10\times$–$100\times$ potency differences (`|\Delta y| \ge 1.0`) map to identical bit vectors.
2. **Binary Hash Collision & Euclidean Norm Distortion:** Folding substructures into a binary 1024-bit vector discards functional-group multiplicity counts, while Euclidean RBF kernels (`SVR(kernel='rbf')`) conflate molecular size with structural similarity.
3. **Matched-Molecular-Pair (MMP) Substituent-Seam Smoothing:** Global similarity kernels smooth predictions across structural neighbors, systematically under-predicting sharp potency jumps induced by single-substituent swaps $R_1 \to R_2$ on a shared core scaffold.

## 2. Constraint-Liberated Seam Surgery (CLSS) Recipe (`how-we-built-it/run_clss_moleculeace.py`)

We designed a single fixed, deterministic, 3-stage CPU-only recipe fit strictly on each target's own stereo-stripped `InChIKey[:14]`-disjoint `split == 'train'` subset (`overlap == 0` across all `30 / 30` benchmarks, after removing the `1` overlapping training stereoisomer in `CHEMBL1871_Ki` and `2` in `CHEMBL204_Ki`):
1. **Stage 1 — Multi-Scale Chiral & Achiral Generalized Tanimoto LOO KRR + 5-Fold Stratified OOF Ensemble:** Extracts multi-radius Chiral and Achiral Morgan bit and count fingerprints ($r \in \{1, 2, 3\}$, `2048` bins), Chiral Pharmacophoric Feature-Morgan (`FCFP`, $r \in \{2, 3\}$), Chiral AtomPair and TopologicalTorsion counts, RDKit path (`minPath=1..7`) + MACCS bits, and 24 stereochemical/physicochemical descriptors (`StandardScaler` fit strictly on `train`). Evaluates 65 closed-form Leave-One-Out (`LOO`) Kernel Ridge Regression experts over exponential-on-Tanimoto Mercer kernels $K_\gamma(x,x') = S(x,x') \exp(-\gamma(1-S(x,x')))$ alongside 5-fold Stratified OOF precomputed-kernel `SVR`, `LightGBM`, and `ExtraTrees`, blended via simplex-constrained `SLSQP` on OOF predictions.
2. **Stage 2 — CLSS Directional Matched-Molecular-Pair (MMP) Substituent-Difference Seam Surgery:** Identifies high-similarity structural neighbor pairs $(i, j)$ inside `split == 'train'` ($S_{\text{achiral}}(x_i, x_j) \ge 0.45$), fits a ridge directional difference operator $\beta_{\text{diff}}$ on $(\phi(x_i) - \phi(x_j)) \mapsto r_i^{\text{OOF}} - r_j^{\text{OOF}}$, and combines multi-bandwidth similarity-weighted neighbor residual smoothers ($p \in \{4, 8, 14\}$) with the directional MMP anchor-shift corrector via OOF simplex optimization.
3. **Stage 3 — Thermometer-BLAS Exact Ruzicka + `ErG` Pharmacophore + 215-Descriptor RankGauss LOO KRR:** Expands integer Morgan counts via unary thermometer thresholds $t \in (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8)$ with step weights $\sqrt{t - t_{\text{prev}}}$ so `float32` BLAS `SGEMM` computes exact $L_1$ MinMax (Ruzicka) intersections, adds `315`-dim `ErG` pharmacophore fingerprints (`ReducedGraphs.GetErGFingerprint`) and `215` 2D RDKit descriptors (`QuantileTransformer(output_distribution='normal')` fit strictly on `train`), and fits final simplex weights strictly on training OOF predictions.
