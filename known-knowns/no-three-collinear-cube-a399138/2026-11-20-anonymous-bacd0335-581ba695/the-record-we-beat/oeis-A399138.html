A399138
a(n) = maximum number of points in an n X n X n grid with no three collinear.
2
1, 8, 16, 28, 40, 64
(list; graph; refs; listen; history; text; internal format)
OFFSET
1,2
COMMENTS
Collinear means collinear as points of R^3 (not modulo n); cf. A280537 for the "no four coplanar" analog.
Lower bounds for the next terms are a(7) >= 73 (attached witness file) and a(8) >= 94 (Kudriashov's linked 94-point configuration). [Updated by Eric W. Weisstein, Oct 27 2026]
a(p) >= p^2 for every prime p, via the graph of an anisotropic binary quadratic form: {(x, y, Q(x,y) mod p) : 0 <= x,y < p} with Q = x^2 - d*y^2, d a quadratic non-residue, contains no three collinear points. For Q = x^2 + y^2 (which requires p == 3 (mod 4)) this is the construction of Por and Wood.
The maximum size of a connected set of points (with each point connected to its six nearest neighbors), no three collinear, is 26. See A393939. - Pontus von Brömssen, Oct 03 2026

FORMULA
a(n) = Theta(n^2): the upper bound a(n) <= 2*n^2 is elementary (each of the n^2 axis-parallel lines carries at most two points; attained at n = 2), the lower bound Omega(n^2) is due to Por and Wood.
EXAMPLE
a(2) = 8: all eight points of the 2 X 2 X 2 cube may be taken, since no line contains three points of that grid.
