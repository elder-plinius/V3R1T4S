# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
A first sweep with a flat CP-SAT model capped at UB - 1 (20 s per instance) found only one improvement and proved 14 listed
UBs optimal. Switching to a hierarchical model (machine choice, then worker choice, plus a redundant cumulative) found
schedules below the listed UB on 95 of the 357 remaining instances in 30 s each, 52 of them proven optimal by CP-SAT.
The large Behnke instances mostly stayed out of reach.
