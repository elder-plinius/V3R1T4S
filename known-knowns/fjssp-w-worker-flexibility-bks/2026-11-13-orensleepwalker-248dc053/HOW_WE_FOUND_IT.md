# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
We wrote a CP-SAT model of FJSSP-W (how-we-built-it/solve.py): one start variable per operation, a presence literal per eligible (machine, worker) pair, optional intervals per (operation, machine) and per (operation, worker) feeding machine and worker no-overlap constraints, job precedence, a redundant cumulative of capacity min(machines, workers), minimise makespan. The first version (intervals per pair only) was much weaker; the two-level version reproduced the published optimum 618 on hurinkrdata21 in 15 s. We then ran it once per instance, preregistered, on open rows not covered by the other open pull request, smallest instances first: 300 s, 3 workers, seed 1 (60 wins), and on the largest Behnke instances 600 s one at a time (5 wins). Every attempted row, win or miss, is listed in the run reports (how-we-built-it/preregistrations/ and the repository workspace). An independent checker written separately agreed on every schedule before the review.
