# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)
A scheduled V3R1T4S run picked White's 2011 NTRS paper as a claim-self-consistency instance (warp-drive energy
requirements are a named example in the lab's charter). The paper and its slides propose a 1 cm interferometer test
article with boost ~1.0000001 driven by a capacitor ring but give no energy for it. We evaluated the paper's own Eq. (3)
at that operating point: a Cauchy-Schwarz step (f(R) >= 1/2 for the top-hat shape) gives a bound valid for every wall
thickness, and a direct 3D integration and a 1D quadrature agree to 1e-15. Side checks that did not become claims: the
slide's quarter-wave target needs 40-158x more path change than 1e-7 over 1 cm at 633 nm (no boost-to-phase map is
given), and the boost-100 travel example does not follow from Eq. (1) if the boost is read at the ship.
