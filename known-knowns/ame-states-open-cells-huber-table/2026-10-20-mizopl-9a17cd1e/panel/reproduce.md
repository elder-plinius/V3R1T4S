> An **Absolutely Maximally Entangled** state $\operatorname{AME}(n, D)$ of $n$ qudits of local dimension $D$ is a normalized pure quantum state $\lvert\Psi\rangle \in (\mathbb{C}^D)^{\otimes n}$ whose reduced density matrix on every subset $S \subset \{0, \dots, n-1\}$ of size $k = \lfloor n / 2 \rfloor$ is maximally mixed:

- Given a symmetric adjacency matrix $\Gamma \in \mathbb{F}_q^{n \times n}$ ($q$ an odd prime, $2^r \le q$) of an $\operatorname{AME}(n, q)$ graph state $\lvert G_q^{(n)}\rangle$ and a shift vector $c \in \mathbb{F}_q^n$ such that $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{F}_q}\begin{pmatrix} \Gamma_{S^c, S} & c_{S^c} \end{pmatrix} = k + 1$ for every $k$-subset $S$ onto which the binary off-diagonal blocks $\operatorname{Tr}_{S^c}(\lvert\psi_\alpha\rangle\langle\psi_\beta\rvert)$ ($\alpha \neq \beta$) do not identically vanish, character orthogonality over $\mathbb{F}_q^{S^c}$ forces $T_{\alpha,\beta}^S = \operatorname{Tr}_{S^c}\bigl(Z_q(\alpha c)\lvert G_q^{(n)}\rangle\langle G_q^{(n)}\rvert Z_q(-\beta c)\bigr) = 0$ for all $\alpha \neq \beta$, while local unitarity of $Z_q(\alpha c)$ yields $T_{\alpha,\alpha}^S = \frac{1}{q^k} I_{q^k}$.
