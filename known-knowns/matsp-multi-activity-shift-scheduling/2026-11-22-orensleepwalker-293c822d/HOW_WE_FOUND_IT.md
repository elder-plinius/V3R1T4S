# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

After two instances fell to zero-penalty rosters, we asked where zero is possible at all. Comparing each instance's minimum total working time with its total maximum cover showed over-staffing is forced on all but three instances, and that the forced excess is itself a lower bound. On 99 instances it equals the best known value; five of those were already proved optimal by the authors of the current values, leaving the 94 certified here.
