# How we built it (contributor text, unverified)

Brief: bank partial win p1 asks for an independent replay of one of the 2026 lower-bound certificates with outward-rounded interval or exact arithmetic. An earlier attempt on this same certificate used binary64 with a hand-proved error bound and lost its criterion skeptic on the arithmetic wording and on sketched lemmas; this run kept the checker's geometry but rebuilt the leaf path on an `Iv` interval type (one-ulp outward widening after every operation), replaced the error theorem by interval coverage checks at every split, and wrote the lemma layer as full proofs. Dead ends: a first witness tier without edge refinement (only 335 of 3,090 sampled leaves pass, doubles cost), bit-twiddled Up/Dn (no speedup), reusing the earlier inflate-based coverage argument (replaced by per-split interval comparisons). The full replay took 149 minutes on 5 threads; a fresh panel then reproduced it. The author of the certificate was not contacted.
