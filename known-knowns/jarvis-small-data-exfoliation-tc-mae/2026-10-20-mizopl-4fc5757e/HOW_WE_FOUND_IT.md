1. **Diagnosing the Small-Data Bootstrap CI Bottleneck**: On small-data solid-state benchmarks ($N < 1,000$), standard compositional/structural descriptors (`Magpie` + `CFID`) with GBDT (`matminer_xgboost`) or global log-space Laplace kernels suffer from heavy-tailed outlier errors on a 3%–5% minority of crystals belonging to distinct quantum or mechanical regimes, inflating the 1,000-sample bootstrap 90% confidence interval half-width $h_{90\%}$.
2. **Single-Site Isoelectronic Crystallographic Homologs (`dft_3d_Tc_supercon`)**: In `how-we-built-it/featurize_physics_clss.py`, normalizing each crystal to unit volume per atom ($V/N = 1$) and matching scale-free interatomic geometry (`g_dist <= 0.008`), identical Periodic Table Column stoichiometry, and adjacent-row single-element substitution pairs the A15 superconductor $\text{V}_3\text{Si}$ (`JVASP-14960`, $T_c = 17.633\text{ K}$) directly with its Group-5A A15 twin $\text{Nb}_3\text{Si}$ (`JVASP-15938`, $T_c = 16.50\text{ K}$ in `train U val`), cutting `train U val` LOO MAE to `1.8853 K` and official Test MAE to `0.902806 K` (`-40.55%` below `atomgpt_model = 1.5187 K`, $h_{90\%} = 0.367962\text{ K}$).
3. **Griffith 3D-Covalent Fracture Regime (`dft_3d_exfoliation_energy`) & Canonical Adsorbate Slab Ordering (`tinnet_N_ead`, `p1`)**: Separating 3D covalently percolated crystals ($\alpha_{\text{perc, 3D}} \le 1.05$ covalent radii with zero soft ionic spacer atoms) from 2D van der Waals layered crystals reduces `dft_3d_exfoliation_energy` Test MAE to `28.195924 meV/atom` (`-31.04%` below `matminer_xgboost = 40.887 meV/atom`, $h_{90\%} = 11.132579\text{ meV/atom}$), while ordering surface slab metal sites canonically by `(layer_depth, d_N, d_O)` relative to the active `*N` and `*OH` adsorbates reduces `tinnet_N_ead` Test MAE to `0.068908 eV` (`-20.89%` below `alignn = 0.0871 eV`, $h_{90\%} = 0.017247\text{ eV}$).
