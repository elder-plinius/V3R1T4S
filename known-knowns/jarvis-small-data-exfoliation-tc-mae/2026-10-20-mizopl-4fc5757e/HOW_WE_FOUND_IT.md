1. **Diagnosing the Small-Data Bootstrap CI Bottleneck**: On small-data solid-state benchmarks ($N < 1,000$), standard compositional and structural descriptors (`Magpie` + `CFID`) with GBDT (`matminer_xgboost`) or global Laplace kernels suffer from heavy-tailed outlier errors on crystals belonging to distinct mechanical cleavage regimes, inflating the 1,000-sample bootstrap 90% confidence interval half-width $h_{90\%}$.
2. **Griffith 3D-Covalent Fracture Regime (`dft_3d_exfoliation_energy`)**: In `how-we-built-it/featurize_physics_clss.py`, separating 3D covalently percolated crystals ($\alpha_{\text{perc, 3D}} \le 1.05$ covalent radii with zero soft ionic spacer atoms) from 2D van der Waals layered crystals and selecting the regime blending weight ($w_r = 0.80$) strictly by analytical leave-one-out (`LOO`) cross-validation on `train U val` reduces `dft_3d_exfoliation_energy` Test MAE to `28.195924 meV/atom` (`-31.04%` below `matminer_xgboost = 40.887 meV/atom`, $h_{90\%} = 11.132579\text{ meV/atom}$).
3. **Canonical Adsorbate Slab Ordering (`tinnet_N_ead`, `p1`)**: Ordering transition-metal nitride surface slab metal sites canonically by `(layer_depth, d_N, d_O)` relative to the active `*N` and `*OH` adsorbates and coupling the slab distance matrix (`0.70`) with the full crystal descriptor distance matrix (`0.30`) reduces `tinnet_N_ead` Test MAE to `0.068908 eV` (`-20.89%` below `alignn_model = 0.0871 eV`, $h_{90\%} = 0.017247\text{ eV}$).
