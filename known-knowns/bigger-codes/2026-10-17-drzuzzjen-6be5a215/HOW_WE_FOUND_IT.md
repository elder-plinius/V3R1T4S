# How we found it (contributor text, unverified)

After five hourly batches of plateau walks of the published V3R1T4S engine (5991 -> 5996, the last two without
gain), we tried kick-regrow: delete R random words from a 5996 code and let the same engine regrow it. Kicks of 25 to
1000 words regrew to 5996 within seconds but back into the same code family (95-100 % of the removed words returned).
Kicks of 2000 words took 4-5 minutes to regrow to 5996 (two of three walks) and, where measured, into a different
family; one of them reached 5997 after 19 minutes. This package holds that code. The idea of perturbing and re-optimising is standard; only its use here is new.
