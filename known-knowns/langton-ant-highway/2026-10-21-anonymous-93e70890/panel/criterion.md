3. Certificates.
   - X (lantx2, METHOD.md section 1): I checked the argument and the code. The induction is sound. E_p = XOR over m=1..p of P0(.+m v); terms with m>M vanish; and K>=M with the sweep beyond B0 gives E_p = E_K. The code's K, M, ring indexing and snapshot invalidation match the argument. X establishes eternal 104-periodic motion with v=(+-2,+-2), and that no cell black at s0 lies in the sweep after period K. That covers p1's three items: the window repeats shifted by v; the ant is beyond all black cells; the future sweep holds no initial black cell.
   - C(s) (lant4): METHOD.md marks its proof only as SKETCH, so I proved it myself. checkC compares G_s(c) = G_s(c+v) XOR P(c+v) on the path translates. By induction, G_{s+104k}(c) = G_s(c) XOR (XOR over m<k of P(c-mv)), so the k-th check is exactly "period k+1 sees the same colours as period k". Once a translate lies wholly beyond the bbox (which includes the box and every position up to t, and so path_0), all later checks hold automatically: G is white there and P is zero there, because P is supported on path_0 inside the bbox. So C(s) is a valid proof of eternal periodicity from s. It is stronger than a literal "window at t = window at t+104" comparison. Window overflow returns -1, never a capture. Minor inaccuracy: METHOD.md says checkC compares the grid "at the detection step t". The code actually rewinds G to time s (it uses the bbox at t, which is harmless). This does not affect soundness.
