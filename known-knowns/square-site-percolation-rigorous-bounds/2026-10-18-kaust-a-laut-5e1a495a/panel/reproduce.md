## 1. Definitions I implemented (quoted from PROOF.md)

- "eps(v) = (upper, lower) in {0,1}^2 records whether (v,1) and (v,0) are open ... each coordinate equal to 1 with
  probability p."
- eta: "upper(min) and upper(max), or lower(min) and lower(max), or upper(min) and lower(max)", where "min [is] the
  endpoint with the smaller coordinate sum".
- "w(beta) ∝ pi(beta) sum_gamma A(gamma, beta) mu_c(gamma)", "mu_c(gamma) = pi(gamma) M_par(gamma) prod_s M_s(gamma)".
- Check: "sum_beta w(beta) (nu_beta(B) - pi_{1-p}(B)) >= 0" for every increasing B.
- Slots: "A vertex whose parent is its min neighbour has child slots (max, max, min). A vertex whose parent is its max
  neighbour has (max, min, min). The root O has (max, max, min, min)."
- Messages from a slot: "all ones, if the slot is absent"; "the eta = 0 leaf message, sum_beta pi(beta)(1 - A_o(., beta)),
  with the sum over all four states"; "for eta = 1, sum_beta pi(beta) A_o(., beta) prod_slots M(beta)"; the parent-side
  message "sum_delta A_o(delta, .) pi(delta) M_par(delta) prod_{other slots} M(delta)", with M_par all ones when the
  parent is O.
- Depth 0: "the subtree beyond the vertex is replaced by an arbitrary nonnegative function of its eps; the resulting
  messages lie in the cone generated by A_o(., beta) over open beta."

## 2. My checker (`../mine/mychk.py`)

The checker works as follows.

- Messages are non-negative integer 3-vectors over the open states of the receiving vertex. Probabilities are scaled
  by the denominator of p, so that pi(e) is an integer. The (0,0) component of every message is always multiplied by
  A(...) = 0, since a closed site has eta = 0 on all its edges, so I drop it.
- Cones are reduced to extreme rays by an exact 2D convex hull of the projective points, using Fractions. The checker
  then verifies that every input generator lies in the returned polygon (or on the segment).
- Recursions:
  - D_0 = hull{ones, leaf_o, A_o(., beta) : beta open}.
  - D_k = hull{ones, leaf_o} together with the images of D_{k-1}^3 over the slots of o.
  - U_0 = hull{A_o(delta, .)}.
  - U_k = the root-parent images (O's other three slots from D_{k-1}) together with the non-root-parent images
    (U_{k-1}^{o'} times the parent's other two slots from D_{k-1}), for both o'.
- Final check: b2 is a max or a min child of c. c = O (three other slots from D_k), or c is a max or a min child
  (U_k times c's two other slots from D_k). All 18 nontrivial increasing events are tested, using exact integer
  numerators.
- The increasing events are enumerated by brute force over all subsets of {0,1}^n. This gives 20 for n = 3 (18
  nontrivial) and 168 for n = 4 (166 nontrivial).

## 3. Proof steps, re-derived

**Lemma 0.** Verified. If theta_S(1-p) = 0, then 1-p <= p_c(S). Otherwise theta_L(p) > 0, so p >= p_c(L) >= p_c(S*),
because site percolation on a subgraph has a larger p_c. Fisher's inequality then gives p_c(S) >= 1 - p. Fisher's
inequality is a cited known result.

**L is contained in S\*.** Verified, by hand and by computer (`../mine/phi.py`):

    bijection OK on box; all 5625 L-edges map to S*-edges; step set [(-1, 0), (-1, 1), (0, 1), (1, 0), (1, 1)]
    missing S* edge types (step: parity of b-a at lower endpoint): {(0, 1): {1}}

So the image is S* with every other vertical edge removed, as stated. Also, an eps-path gives an open L-path: when the
path enters a site through one open copy and leaves through the other, both copies are open and are joined by the
vertical edge.

**Lemma 1 (coupling).** Verified.

- At the root, P(xi(O) = 1) = 1 - (1-p)^2 >= 1 - p.
- At each step, Strassen's theorem (Theorem 1.3) gives Q_h on {zeta <= xi}. Drawing zeta from Q_h(. | xi) makes
  (xi, zeta) on the children have law Q_h given the past. The past zeta values depend only on the past xi values and
  on auxiliary uniforms that are independent of eps. So each new batch is Ber(1-p)^children given everything before it.
- The hybrid-field induction: the swapped set is a function of the history, and the fresh Y values on it are
  independent. So each hybrid field is i.i.d., and so is the pointwise limit (its finite-dimensional laws).
- Die-out case: every neighbour of a vertex with xi = 1 has received a xi-value, with zeta <= xi there. Induction
  along a zeta-open path from O therefore keeps xi = 1, and the zeta-cluster of O is finite.
- Survival case: infinitely many distinct processed vertices, all eps-connected to O. This gives an infinite L-cluster
  through (O,0) or (O,1), and so theta_S(1-p) <= 2 theta_L(p).
- The die-out/survival case split is correct and necessary.

**Lemma 2 (tree information).** Verified.

- Selection, the children and stopping depend only on which vertices have xi-values, the xi-values themselves and the
  fixed order. So {history = h} is exactly {eps(O) open} together with {eta(e) = x_e on the tree edges}, and the
  posterior factorises as stated.
- b2 has not been processed yet, so it is a leaf of T with only the edge to c.
- Its children are not in T, so their eps are fresh. Non-tree edges at b2 are never revealed.
- The 1[eps(O) open] factor is omitted from the displayed density. At any step after the first it is implied by an
  eta = 1 tree edge at O, because A(00, .) = 0, so this is harmless.

**Cones and multilinearity.** Verified.

- The margin is linear in each message, and the normaliser is positive.
- A function that is linear in one message and nonnegative on that message's generators is nonnegative on its cone.
  Induction over the messages finishes the argument.
- The worst normalised margin over a product of cones is attained at generator tuples, because the margin is
  linear-fractional in each message separately.
- Every real message lies in the stated cones, by induction on k:
  - the depth-0 cone covers any F >= 0 beyond the cut;
  - shallower subtrees (absent slots, leaves, unprocessed eta = 1 children) are generators or lie in the cones;
  - c's parent edge has eta = 1, because c was processed.
- All real cases are modelled: c = O, or c is a max or a min child; b2 is a max or a min child; each slot is absent,
  a leaf, or eta = 1. Geometric constraints (sites must be distinct) are dropped, which only enlarges the set.

**Outward rounding soundness.** Verified (window.py `round_out`, read after my checker was written).

- Each normalised generator u is floored to f on the 2^-40 grid (`int()` of a non-negative Fraction is a floor), with
  r = 1 - sum f >= 0.
- The vertices t_i = f + r e_i satisfy u = sum_i (d_i / r) t_i, where d = u - f >= 0 and sum d = r. So u lies in
  their convex hull, and the new cone contains the old one.
- All of this is exact Fraction arithmetic. The hull keys are Fractions, so there is no floating point anywhere in
  window.py's check.

**Root step.** Verified independently: 166 nontrivial increasing events of {0,1}^4, worst margin 1.470118695e-02 > 0,
with posterior pi restricted to the open states and slots (max, max, min, min). P(xi(O) = 1) - (1-p) = 0.13338511 > 0.

**Fewer than three children.** Verified. Imaginary children with fresh eps in the missing slots make the three-slot law
sum_beta w(beta) nu_beta, and the real children's law is its marginal. An increasing event of the real coordinates is
a cylinder increasing event of the three slots, so domination passes to the marginal. The brute force above includes
2-child cases.
