# How We Built It: 4-Level Matryoshka Functional ANOVA + Universal Kriging TKRR (`Track A`, Partial Win `p1`)

## 1. Chronological Pre-Test Validation & Training Scripts (`how-we-built-it/`)

All scripts below use `BASE_DIR = Path(__file__).resolve().parent` and operate directly on the official TDC `BindingDB_Patent` dataset (<https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/4798216>, `dti_dg_group.zip` `sha256: 4708233b8536d856a9ee6522623e4d44bbf7d2efc1c8496e69f6cd2c6482457a`):

### Phase 0 & 1 — Pre-Test `train_val` Inner Temporal Validation (`step01`–`step07`) and First 5-Seed Evaluation (`step08`)
1. `step01_download_manifest_and_eda.py` (`2026-10-29 03:12`): Downloads `BindingDB_Patent` (`train_val.csv`: `183,430` rows, `2013–2018`; `test.csv`: `49,028` rows, `2019–2021`), verifies SHA-256 commitments (filtering out `.DS_Store` and `__MACOSX`), and computes domain-shift EDA.
2. `step02_inner_temporal_anatomy.py` (`2026-10-29 04:12` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Defines **Inner Temporal Split A (`2013–2016 -> 2017–2018`, $n=58,283$)** and **Inner Temporal Split B (`2013–2017 -> 2018`, $n=24,499$)** strictly inside `train_val` (`2013–2018`). Evaluates smoothed target mean (`0.45340` Split A, `0.45172` Split B) and target + drug residual (`0.50615` Split A, `0.50157` Split B).
3. `step03_precompute_features.py` (`2026-10-29 04:13`): Precomputes CPU-only protein descriptors (`586` dims: AAC, DPC, CTD, physicochemical) and packed binary ligand fingerprints (`2048-bit ECFP4`, `1024-bit ECFP6`, `1024-bit FCFP4`, `167-bit MACCS`, `40` RDKit 2D descriptors).
4. `step03_test_target_ligand_kernel_on_temporal_val.py` (`2026-10-29 04:13` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Tests target-conditioned ligand Tanimoto transfer and protein k-mer homology on Inner Temporal Split A.
5. `numba_dual_kernel.py` & `step04_test_dual_kernel_splits.py` (`2026-10-29 04:26` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Numba-JIT bit-parallel Tanimoto kernel engine benchmarked on Inner Splits A and B.
6. `step05_test_tkrr_and_cold_models.py` (`2026-10-29 04:29` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Introduces **Per-Target Multi-View Tanimoto Kernel Ridge Regression (`TKRR-Mix(1,2,4)`)**, jumping Inner Split A PCC to **`0.57503`** and Inner Split B PCC to **`0.55863`**.
7. `step05b_test_homology_tkrr.py` (`2026-10-29 04:35` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Introduces **Schur-Complement RKHS Leverage $\tau_{\text{same}} = \|L_t^{-1} k_t(d)\|_2^2$** to gate same-target TKRR with top-$K$ homologous-target TKRR (`0.57548` Split A, `0.56207` Split B).
8. `step06b_inspect_unseen_target_homology.py` (`2026-10-29 04:41` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Introduces **Catalytic Pocket-Core Local Alignment (`S_pocket`)** and containment alignment (`S_contain`) via 5-mer diagonal voting + banded BLOSUM62 Smith-Waterman normalized by a 210-AA catalytic domain self-score.
9. `step06_test_universal_kriging_and_unseen.py` (`2026-10-29 04:44` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Combines **Level 1 (LOTO Target KRR) + Level 2 (Global CG Ligand Potency Ridge + Tanimoto Kernel) + Level 3 (Factored Bilinear Kronecker Interaction + Pocket-Homology Transfer) + Level 4 (Universal Kriging Multi-View TKRR on Global-Prior Residuals)**, jumping Inner Split A PCC to **`0.60856`** and Inner Split B PCC to **`0.58805`**.
10. `proofer2_fast_homology_and_gbdt.py` & `step07_test_full_modular_stack_splits.py` (`2026-10-29 05:20` — **Pre-Test `train_val` Only**): Validates the complete 4-level CPU-only stacker (`Track A`) on Inner Temporal Split A (**`0.62386`**) and Inner Temporal Split B (**`0.60613`**) before any test evaluation.
11. `step08_run_5seeds_and_verify.py` (`2026-10-29 05:24` — **Test Evaluation #1, Claimed Primary `Track A` for `p1`**): Trains and evaluates the frozen `step07` CPU-only architecture (`Track A`: `0.580 ± 0.003` [`0.57942 ± 0.00299`]), `Track B` (`0.57106 ± 0.00260`), and `ERM` (`0.51310 ± 0.00271`) across official seeds `1..5`, writing keyed `_part1.csv` (`rows 0..24,513`) and `_part2.csv` (`rows 24,514..49,027`) with columns `row_index,Drug_ID,Target_ID,y_pred`.
