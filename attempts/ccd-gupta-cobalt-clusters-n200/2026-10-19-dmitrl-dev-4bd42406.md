## What was tried

- Neighbour seeding: etch the best N+1 structure by each atom of coordination below 10 and grow the best N-1 structure over
  up to 120 convex-hull facets, relax, cascade, for every N = 3..200; then growth with three- and four-fold sites for
  N = 101..200.
- Basin hopping: monotonic, 250 steps per size from the best structure, N = 200 down to 180 (stopped there), relocating 1 to 3
  least-coordinated atoms, with a Gaussian shake of all atoms (sigma 0.08 r0) in 10% of the steps.
- Decahedral family: etch the decahedral Co.zip improvements (N = 95, 101, 113, 121, 196, 200) atom by atom, up to six steps,
  and grow them with three-fold, then three- and four-fold sites.

## Why it fell short

- One-atom moves from the best neighbours never went below the table. The closest misses are N = 177 (0.000578 eV above)
  and N = 161 (0.001790 eV above); 64 of the 100 sizes N = 101..200 are reproduced exactly by growing the best N-1
  structure, so the posted structures form a consistent growth sequence under this move.
- The Co.zip improvements do not carry to their neighbours by one atom (N = 96 from Co.zip 95 plus one atom is 0.0847 eV
  above the table).
- Monotonic basin hopping accepted no move at any of the 21 sizes; a Metropolis walk or lattice-site moves would be needed.
- The decahedral family ties or loses at every size reached, although its moves pass their positive controls (196 to 197
  and 121 to 122 reach the Co.zip energies exactly); it loses 4.2 to 4.4 eV per removed atom against 3.8 to 4.0 for the table.
- Not run in this budget: Metropolis basin hopping or lattice searches at N = 177 and N = 161.
