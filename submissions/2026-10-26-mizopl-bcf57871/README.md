# Conway's 99-Graph Problem (`conway-99-graph`, Tracks `p2` and `p1`)

## What

Dual-encoded DRAT and pure-RUP LRAT (0 RAT lemmas) machine-checked UNSAT certificates across two independent SAT cardinality encodings (Encoding A = Sinz 2005 Sequential Counter, Encoding B = Bailleux & Boufkhad 2003 Binary Tree Totalizer) for (1) all 5 forced-structure reduction propositions (p2_1 through p2_5) of Thakkar & Severini (arXiv:2608.11211), including positive SAT non-vacuity on srg(9, 4, 1, 2) = K_3 box K_3 (Track p2), and (2) 8 prime-order and subgroup automorphism exclusions on srg(99, 14, 1, 2) fixing a root vertex x: prime order p=3 with 33 fixed vertices and 22 3-cycles (p3_t1_fix33), 6 prime-order p=2 involutions with 73, 53, 51, 21, 19, and 11 fixed vertices (p2_a0_b1_c6_fix73, p2_a1_b0_c5_fix53, p2_a0_b2_c5_fix51, p2_a1_b2_c3_fix21, p2_a0_b4_c3_fix19, p2_a1_b3_c2_fix11), and the order-21 Frobenius group Frob(21) = Z_7 x| Z_3 generated by prime orders p=7 (1 fixed vertex, 14 7-cycles) and p=3 (3 fixed vertices, 32 3-cycles) (frob21_z7_z3) (Track p1), verified from freshly generated CNF clauses in ~11 seconds by a standalone pure-Python LRAT RUP proof checker.

## Check It Yourself

Run the standalone standard-library verifier from the root of this package:

```sh
sh verify-it-yourself/run_checks.sh
```

It finishes in $\sim 11$ seconds using only the Python standard library (`hashlib`, `itertools`, `json`, `os`, `sys`), re-generating all 26 CNF formulas from scratch across Encoding A and Encoding B, checking the positive $\mathrm{srg}(9, 4, 1, 2) \cong K_3 \square K_3$ SAT control, verifying exact clause membership of all 26 trimmed `*_core.cnf` files against the freshly generated CNFs, verifying all SHA-256 digests against `object/certificate.json`, and checking all 19,693 Reverse Unit Propagation (RUP) steps (`0 RAT lemmas`) across the 26 LRAT proofs to the empty clause `0`.

## Compared To

- **Thakkar & Severini (`arXiv:2608.11211v2`, 18 Sep 2026):** Derived the forced-structure reduction around a fixed vertex $x \in V(\Gamma)$ for $\Gamma = \mathrm{srg}(99, 14, 1, 2)$ and checked it via CP-SAT/backtracking without published SAT CNF encodings or DRAT/LRAT proof certificates. Here all 5 reduction propositions (`p2_1`–`p2_5`) are certified with trimmed CNF cores, DRAT proofs, and pure-RUP LRAT proofs (`0 RAT lemmas`) across two structurally distinct SAT cardinality encodings.
- **Crnković, Maksimović & Švob (`arXiv:2308.02978`, 2023) and Wilbrink (1983):** Excluded automorphisms of prime orders $p = 3$ and $p = 2$ and the Frobenius group $\mathrm{Frob}(21) = \mathbb{Z}_7 \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_3$ via custom backtracking/ILP/GAP scripts without machine-checkable DRAT/LRAT UNSAT proof certificates. Here 8 distinct prime-order and Frobenius automorphism actions (`p3_t1_fix33`, 6 involutions `p2_*`, and `frob21_z7_z3`) are certified UNSAT with DRAT and pure-RUP LRAT proofs (`0 RAT lemmas`) across both Encoding A and Encoding B.

## Not Claimed

- We do not resolve the full existence or non-existence of Conway's 99-graph $\mathrm{srg}(99, 14, 1, 2)$ in the asymmetry case ($|\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma)| = 1$), nor do we provide a single monolithic LRAT proof for the remaining deepest involution branches (`(0,3,4)`, `(2,0,3)`, `(3,1,0)`, etc.) whose solver runs exceed lightweight standalone package budgets.
